## Understanding N-P-K

Every fertilizer bag shows **three numbers** (e.g., 32-0-6):
- **N (Nitrogen)**: First number → green leaf growth
- **P (Phosphorus)**: Second number → root development (supports seed germination)
- **K (Potassium)**: Third number → overall health, cold hardiness, drought resistance

### Example: 32-0-6 Fertilizer, 20 lb Bag
- Nitrogen: 32% × 20 lb = 6.4 lb nitrogen
- Phosphorus: 0%
- Potassium: 6% × 20 lb = 1.2 lb potassium

## Slow-Release vs. Quick-Release

### Slow-Release (Recommended) ✅
- Releases nitrogen gradually over 8–16 weeks
- Low burn risk
- Does not wash away easily in rain
- Look for "Slow-release," "Extended feeding," or "Controlled release" on packaging
- Price: Slightly more expensive

### Quick-Release
- Immediate greening effect
- Risk of burn if over-applied or used in dry conditions
- Can wash out in heavy rain (wasteful)
- Price: Cheaper

## Application Method and Precautions

### Pre-Application Checklist
- [ ] Check recommended application rate on package (lbs/1,000 sq ft)
- [ ] Calculate your lawn area (0.3 acres = approx. 13,000 sq ft)
- [ ] Confirm spreader setting (package shows settings by model)

### Application Timing (Important!)
- **Best**: Apply 1–2 days before rain forecast → absorbed through natural watering
- **Acceptable**: Water immediately after application (30+ minutes)
- **Avoid**: High summer heat (30°C+), fast burn on dry days
- **Avoid**: When grass is diseased or stressed

### Burn Prevention
- Never exceed recommended rate (more is not better)
- If applying in morning dew, water immediately after (dew can concentrate the product)
- Use slow-release type

## Calculations for My Lawn (0.3 Acres)

**Area**: 0.3 acres × 43,560 sq ft/acre = approx. 13,000 sq ft

### Fertilizer Quantity Example (Scotts 32-0-6 Slow-Release)
- Recommended rate: 2.5 lbs per 1,000 sq ft
- Amount needed: 13,000 ÷ 1,000 × 2.5 = **32.5 lbs**
- Purchase: One 40 lb bag is sufficient

### Scotts Turf Builder Spreader Settings
- Scotts EdgeGuard: Setting 4 (always verify on package)
- Direction: Apply in horizontal rows then crosswise vertically for even coverage
