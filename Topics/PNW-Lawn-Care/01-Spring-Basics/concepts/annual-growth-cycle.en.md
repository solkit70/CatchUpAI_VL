# Cool-Season Lawn Annual Growth Cycle

**Location**: Tehaleh, WA (USDA Zone 8b)
**Grass Type**: Cool-season (Tall Fescue / Perennial Ryegrass / Kentucky Bluegrass)
**Created**: 2026-03-03

## Sprinkler Seasonal Adjust Annual Guide

| Month | Seasonal Adjust | Watering Frequency | Reason |
|-------|----------------|--------------------|--------|
| Jan–Feb | 0% (OFF) | — | PNW rainfall sufficient |

| May | 80–100% | 2x/week | Growth + increasing dryness |
| Jun | 100–120% | 3x/week | Entering dry season |

## Seasonal Core Tasks Detail

### 🌱 Spring (March–May) — Busiest Season

**March (Now!)**:
- [ ] Apply pre-emergent herbicide (before soil reaches 10°C)
- [ ] First fertilizer application (high nitrogen, slow-release)
- [ ] First mow (don't cut too short — maintain 7–8 cm)
- [ ] Lawn survey (identify thin areas, weed locations)
- [ ] Check if Dethatching is needed

**April**:
- [ ] Aerating (Core Aeration — requires rental)
- [ ] Overseeding (if pre-emergent was not applied)
- [ ] 2nd fertilizer application (if needed)
- [ ] Start sprinklers when dry spells arrive

**May**:
- [ ] Increase mowing frequency (1-2x/week)
- [ ] Start sprinklers 2x/week
- [ ] Set Seasonal Adjust to 80–100%

---

### ☀️ Summer (June–August) — Watering Is Key

**June**:
- [ ] Seasonal Adjust 100–120%
- [ ] Weed removal (post-emergent herbicide may be used)
- [ ] Raise mowing height (7–10 cm) — shade effect protects moisture

**July–August (Most Critical!)**:
- [ ] Watering time: **4–8 AM** (minimize evaporation)
- [ ] Watering goal: 1–1.5 inches per week
- [ ] Deep and infrequent (25 min every other day beats 10 min daily)
- [ ] No fertilizer (burn risk)
- [ ] Brown color = dormancy (normal) — do not stop watering

---

### 🍂 Fall (September–October) — Second Golden Season

**September–October (Best time for Overseeding!)**:
- [ ] Core Aerating
- [ ] Overseeding (more recommended than spring — long growth period after germination)
- [ ] Fall fertilizer (high potassium — 0-0-20 or 24-0-11)
- [ ] Weed management (weeds are also active this season)
- [ ] Gradually reduce Seasonal Adjust

---

### ❄️ Winter (November–December) — System Protection

**November (before frost)**:
- [ ] Sprinkler Winterizing (Blow Out)
- [ ] Rain Bird ESP-ME3 OFF or Seasonal Adjust 0%
- [ ] Remove fallen leaves (leaves piled on grass will rot)

---

## Watering Golden Rules

1. **Deep and infrequent** — 30 min every other day grows deeper roots than 10 min daily
2. **Water at dawn** — 4–8 AM is optimal. Evening watering risks fungal disease
3. **Check the soil** — Water when soil 5 cm deep is dry
4. **Footprint test** — If footprints remain 10 minutes after stepping, the lawn is thirsty
