## 🌿 April–May (Lawn Care)

### First Mow of the Season
- [ ] **Timing**: When grass reaches 4 inches height (Cool-season recommended height: 3–3.5 inches)
- [ ] **Blade Check**: Use sharp blades (dull blades cause brown tips)
- [ ] **First Mow Height**: Do not cut more than 1/3 at once (target 3.5 inches → mow when over 5 inches)
- [ ] **Grass Clippings**: Return as mulch (natural fertilizer effect)

### Weed Monitoring
- [ ] **Mid-April**: Confirm pre-emergent effectiveness — check for weed germination
- [ ] **If Found**: Manual removal or spot treatment (consider surrounding grass)

## ☀️ June–July (Summer Prep)

### Irrigation Schedule Transition
- [ ] **Increase Frequency**: 3x/week → daily (for July–August dry season)
- [ ] **Deep Watering**: Target moisture penetration to 6-inch soil depth
- [ ] **ESP-ME3 Seasonal Adjustment**: Set Seasonal Adjust feature to +20–30%

### Summer Fertilizer
- [ ] **Timing**: Early June
- [ ] **Product**: Low-nitrogen Slow-release (minimize summer stress)
- [ ] **Caution**: Do not apply on hot days (grass burn risk)

## 🍂 September (Core Fall Tasks)

### Overseeding (Thin Areas in Front Yard)
- [ ] **Confirm Pre-emergent Effect Has Worn Off**: At least 8–10 weeks elapsed (based on April application → cleared by July)
- [ ] **Aerate First**: Core Aerating to relieve soil compaction
  - Equipment rental: Home Depot (~$80/day)
  - Full back yard + full front yard
- [ ] **Seed Selection**: Tall Fescue (to match existing grass)
  - Jonathan Green Black Beauty Ultra recommended
  - Thin areas of front yard: approx. 2,000 sq ft expected
- [ ] **Seed Application**: Immediately after aerating (so seeds fall into holes)
- [ ] **Starter Fertilizer**: Apply immediately after seeding
- [ ] **Increase Irrigation**: Shallow watering 2–3x daily for 2 weeks after seeding

### Fall Fertilizer
- [ ] **Timing**: Early October
- [ ] **Product**: Scotts WinterGuard (high potassium P-K, winter preparation)

---

## ❄️ November (Winter Preparation)

### Final Mow
- [ ] **Height**: 2.5–3 inches (too short risks freeze damage, too long risks Snow Mold)
- [ ] **Date**: Final mow before first frost

### Sprinkler Winterizing
- [ ] **System Blow-Out**: Remove water from pipes with compressed air (prevent freeze damage)
  - Professional service recommended: $80–120
  - Or DIY: Rent air compressor (40–50 PSI max)
- [ ] **Switch Controller to Off or Rain Mode**
