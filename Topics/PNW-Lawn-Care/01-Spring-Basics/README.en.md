## Key Lessons from This Module

### Lesson 1: In PNW, you prepare your lawn in spring — for summer
PNW summers (July–August) bring almost no rain and high heat. Cool-season grasses face serious stress or go dormant. Building strong roots in spring (March–April) is the key to summer survival.

### Lesson 2: Spring task order matters
Pre-emergent → Fertilizer → (Fall) Aerating → Overseeding
After Pre-emergent, overseeding is NOT possible for 8–10 weeks!

### Lesson 3: Mid-March is the golden window
- Pre-emergent: Before soil reaches 10°C = right now!
- Spring fertilizer: Just before active growth = right now!
- Lawn audit: Before spring tasks begin = right now!
