## 🚶 Step-by-Step Application Guide

### Step 1: Set Up Spreader (10 min)

1. Find your spreader brand on the back of the bag
2. Adjust the spreader gate to the correct setting
3. **Start slightly below the recommended setting** (to prevent over-application)
4. Pour product from bag into spreader (recommended to fill in halves)

---

### Step 2: Plan Your Application Path (5 min)

```
Recommended order: Back yard first, then front yard

Back yard application pattern:
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ → → → → → → → → → → → →  │
│ ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← │
│ → → → → → → → → → → → →  │
│ ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← ← │
└─────────────────────────────┘
      (walk back and forth horizontally)

Then rotate 90° and apply vertically:
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑ ↓ ↑ │
└─────────────────────────────┘
      (horizontal + vertical cross = even application)
```

> **Cross Pattern**: Once horizontal, once vertical → even distribution
> Note: When cross-applying, **cut the spreader setting in half** to keep total amount correct

---

### Step 3: Apply (30–45 min)

**Precautions while applying:**

1. Walk at a **consistent pace** — slow walking means over-application
2. **Keep handle lever engaged while moving** — releasing stops application
3. **Lawn edges**: If spreader has EdgeGuard, turn ON → blocks product toward flower beds/beds
4. **Avoid overlapping**: Move sideways by the width of the spreader wheel
5. **Don't stop**: Stopping in one spot causes over-application → can burn grass

**Application Order:**
```
1. Walk perimeter of back yard first (edges first)
2. Apply back yard interior in horizontal strips
3. (Optional) Apply back yard in vertical strips (set to half setting)
4. Walk perimeter of front yard
5. Apply front yard interior
```

---

### Step 4: Post-Application Cleanup (10 min)

1. **Water**: Run sprinklers for **15–20 minutes** after application
   - Pre-emergent must soak into the soil to be effective
   - Natural rain is fine (avoid heavy downpour which risks runoff)
2. **Clean Spreader**: Rinse with water and store (leftover product corrodes metal)
3. **Wash Hands**: Thoroughly with soap
4. **Store Bag**: Seal remaining product and store in a cool, dry place (garage is fine)

## ⚠️ Things You Must Never Do

| ❌ Prohibited | Reason |
|--------------|--------|
| Spread new seed (Overseeding) | Pre-emergent blocks ALL seeds — including lawn seed |
| Seed within 8–10 weeks after application | Pre-emergent still active → seeds won't germinate |
| Apply right before heavy rain | Granules wash away → no effect, environmental pollution |
| Over-apply | Grass can be burned or weakened |
| Apply to flower beds/garden beds | Will also block new plant seedlings |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: There are white granules visible on the grass after application. Is this normal?**
A: Yes, completely normal. They will disappear into the soil after watering.

**Q: Can children or pets go on the lawn after application?**
A: After watering, once the grass is completely dry it's fine. Usually 24 hours later.

**Q: My front yard borders the road — what do I do at the edges?**
A: If the spreader has EdgeGuard, turn it ON → it blocks application toward the road/sidewalk. Without it, skip or hand-apply the last 2 feet along the border.

**Q: What if it rains right after I apply?**
A: Light rain (under 0.5 inches) is fine or even beneficial. Heavy downpour (over 1 inch) risks runoff.

**Q: How long does the effect last?**
A: Scotts Halts lasts **3–4 months** (blocks most weeds through mid-June)
