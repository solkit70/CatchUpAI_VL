## 🌱 Understanding the Two Spring Essentials

Spring lawn care requires two critical tasks:

| Task | Role | Timing |
|------|------|--------|
| **Pre-emergent Application** | Block weed seeds (Crabgrass, etc.) before they germinate | Early spring (before 3–4 mows) |
| **Spring Fertilizer Application** | Nourish lawn after winter, promote fast greening | Simultaneously with pre-emergent, or 4 weeks after |

> ⚠️ **Timing matters**: Pre-emergent must be applied **before** weed seeds germinate. Apply before temperatures regularly reach 80°F (27°C), or before 3–4 mows.

## ⚠️ Common Mistake: Accidentally Buying DiseaseEx

> "Scotts DiseaseEx Lawn Fungicide" is **not** a pre-emergent or fertilizer.

- **Role**: A **fungicide** for treating and preventing fungal diseases (Brown Patch, Pythium Blight, etc.)
- **When needed**: Only when your lawn shows signs of disease
- **Not needed for general spring maintenance** — watch out for this mistake
