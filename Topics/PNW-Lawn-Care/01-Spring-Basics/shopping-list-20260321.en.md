## 🌿 Iron Effect Comparison (Reference)

| Method | Greening Speed | Duration |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Liquid Iron | 24–48 hours | 4–6 weeks |
| Granular Iron (Ironite) | 2–3 weeks | 6–8 weeks |
| Spring Fertilizer (Nitrogen) | 3–4 weeks | 8–12 weeks |

> Iron = fast color change only / Fertilizer = long-term growth from roots
> **Combining both is most effective**
