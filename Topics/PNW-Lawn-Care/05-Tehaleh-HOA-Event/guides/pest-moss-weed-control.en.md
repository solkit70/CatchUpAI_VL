# Pest, Moss & Weed Control Guide

## Pests

### Grubs
- **Damage**: Eat roots → lawn can be pulled up like a carpet
- **Symptoms**: Brown patches in specific areas, lawn lifts like a carpet, white C-shaped larvae found in soil
- **Secondary damage**: Raccoons and birds dig up lawn looking for grubs
- **Treatment**:
  - **Beneficial Nematodes**: Eco-friendly treatment option
  - **Insecticide**: Grub insecticide from Home Depot → effective within 2 weeks
  - Follow up with overseeding to restore the damaged area

> "Raccoons go in and start digging for the grubs, start digging for worms. Whatever they're eating, fix it in. If you have a lot of grubs, the raccoons will go away eventually."

### Chinch Bugs
- Live around roots, suck plant juices
- **Symptoms**: Yellow-brown patches starting in sun-exposed areas
- **Treatment**: **Insecticidal Soap** or targeted insecticide spray

### Wildlife Damage
- **Raccoons**: Dig up lawn looking for grubs/worms
- **Moles**: Create tunnels causing lawn to heave
- **Voles**: Create surface runways across the lawn
- **Response**: Eliminate grubs and the food source disappears, animals leave naturally

## Moss

### Conditions That Cause Moss
- Shade, excess moisture, poor air circulation → serious problem in Tehaleh winters
- When grass isn't kept short → moisture accumulates under blades → moss thrives

> "In winter, your last cuts are pretty low. You don't want a bunch of shadows and a lot of moisture and water being held up below the blades, because that's when you get moss."

### Tehaleh Moss Situation
> "We've had the worst winter that I've seen in years for moss this year. Most of the yards that are in the bottom have the worst ones."

### Moss Removal Method
1. Apply **Moss Killer** → weakens the moss
2. **Dethatch** to remove it (same machine)
3. Dethatching after moss killer increases effectiveness

> "Is that good to do after you put some moss killer on? Yeah. It releases it and kills it, so it's not grabbing as hard."

### Moss Prevention
- Mow low before winter (eliminate shade/moisture buildup)
- Improve drainage with Aeration
- Trim tree branches that create excessive shade

## Weeds

### POA Annua (Most Troublesome Weed)
- The most common and difficult-to-remove weed in Tehaleh
- **Characteristics**: Spreads wide, looks like a large discolored patch in the lawn
- **Removal**: Sprays have almost no effect → **digging it out** is the only solution

> "The only thing you can do to fix it is cut it out on your lawn. It's the only fix."

### General Weed Control
- Thick lawn = best weed defense (no space for weeds to grow)
- Apply pre-emergent in late spring to suppress weed seed germination
- **Caution with herbicides in summer**: Stressed grass may also be damaged

> "Don't treat weeds in summer because you don't want to stress your grass to kill weeds — it will kill the grass and it won't get back."

### Weed Germination Conditions
- Excess moisture → promotes seed germination
- Seeds blow in from common areas and neighboring lawns
- Wet surface promotes seed germination → **morning watering** keeps surface dry during the day

## Fungal Diseases

### Red Thread

> "I think everybody's lawn right now, through the winter, has gotten red thread because we've had so much rain."

- **Causes**: Excess moisture, evening watering, **nitrogen deficiency** (especially common on Tehaleh's low-nitrogen lawns)
- **Symptoms**: Pink-red thread-like fungal strands at blade tips → irregular yellow-brown patches
- **Treatment**: Dual treatment with Fungicide + nitrogen fertilizer
- Widespread in Tehaleh during spring 2026

### Dollar Spot
- **Symptoms**: Straw-colored patches the size of a dollar coin → merges into larger patches if left untreated
- Triggered by nitrogen deficiency + moisture stress

### Powdery Mildew
- **Symptoms**: White powdery coating on grass blades
- Mainly occurs in shaded and moist areas

### Lawn Rust
- **Causes**: Excess moisture, fungus (different from iron fertilizer rust)
- **Symptoms**: Yellow/orange powder on grass blades → stains shoes
- **Treatment**: Fungicide

> "Rust is a fungus, and you treat it with a fungicide."

---

## Shade Problems (Excess Shade)

- **Symptoms**: Thinning and weakening grass, powdery mildew
- **Treatment**:
  - Trim branches to increase sunlight
  - **Overseed with shade-tolerant blend** (separate mix for shaded areas)

---

## General Prevention Principles

From slide: *"Apply Fungicide Preventatively Each Spring."*

| Problem | Prevention |
|---------|-----------|
| Moss | Mow low before winter, aerate, reduce shade |
| Fungus | Morning watering, prevent overwatering, **apply preventive fungicide every spring** |
| Weeds | Maintain thick lawn, apply pre-emergent at the right time in spring |
| Grubs | Beneficial Nematodes or insecticide + overseed to restore |
| POA | Dig out and overseed immediately |
| Shade | Trim branches or overseed with shade-tolerant variety |

> "Once you do pull out those sample diseases and stuff, make sure you overseed it, because if there's another spot for it to go back into, it's going to just come back again."
