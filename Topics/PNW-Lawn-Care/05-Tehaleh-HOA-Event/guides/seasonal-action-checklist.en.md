# Seasonal Action Checklist

## Spring (March – May)

### 🌱 Top Priorities
- [ ] **Apply Moss Killer** → Dethatch 2 weeks later
- [ ] **First fertilizer application** (early March) — Nitrogen (N) focused starter fertilizer (e.g., 30-0-4)
- [ ] **Restart sprinklers** — inspect heads, begin seasonal adjust settings
- [ ] **Apply Pre-emergent** — before weed seeds germinate

### Mowing
- [ ] Set height to 2.5 inches (middle notch)
- [ ] Replace or sharpen blade (must do once per year in spring)
- [ ] Start at 1–2x/week → increase to 2–3x/week as growth accelerates

### Watering
- [ ] 2–3 times/week, weekly goal of 1 inch
- [ ] Set timer for 4–6 AM
- [ ] Verify Rain Sensor is working

### Soil Management
- [ ] Aeration (choose spring or fall)
- [ ] Topdressing: Apply 1/4 inch compost after aeration
- [ ] Soil pH test (recommended every 2–3 years, $100) — target pH 6.0–7.0
- [ ] If pH is low: **Apply lime**

### Fungus Prevention
- [ ] **Apply preventive fungicide in spring** — apply every spring as a preventive measure

### Important Notes
- [ ] Do not overseed within 8–10 weeks after pre-emergent application
- [ ] Spring overseeding is exposed to summer stress → fall is preferred

---

## Summer (June – August)

### ☀️ Top Priorities
- [ ] **Increase watering**: June 80% → July–August 100–110% (Seasonal Adjust)
- [ ] **Maintain fertilizer schedule**: Once each in June and July–August (nitrogen focused)

### Mowing
- [ ] Raise height to 3–4 inches to protect roots
- [ ] Continue at least 1x/week (follow 1/3 rule)
- [ ] During extended heat above 90°F: prioritize watering and root survival first

### Watering
- [ ] 3–5 times/week, weekly goal of 1.5–2 inches
- [ ] In extreme heat, watering more than once a day is acceptable
- [ ] Use cup test to measure actual water output

### Weed Management
- [ ] **Caution with herbicides**: Stressed grass may also be damaged by weed killer
- [ ] Dig out POA immediately when spotted (sprays have no effect)

### Overseeding (Optional)
- [ ] August–September is optimal → start preparing toward end of summer
- [ ] Mow as low as possible before overseeding
- [ ] Increase watering to 3–5 times/day during germination period

---

## Fall (September – October)

### 🍂 Top Priorities
- [ ] **Aeration** — recommended once per year; **fall is optimal**
- [ ] **Overseeding** (immediately after aeration)
- [ ] **Fall fertilizer** (switch to product with higher Potassium K ratio)

### Mowing
- [ ] Gradually lower height (preparing for winter)
- [ ] Reduce frequency as grass growth slows

### Watering
- [ ] Reduce Seasonal Adjust to 80%
- [ ] 2 times/week, weekly goal of 1 inch
- [ ] Apply exception during overseeding period: 3–5 times/day

### Weed Management
- [ ] **Fall weed control** (less risky than summer)
- [ ] After overseeding, confirm new grass is established → no herbicides

### Sprinklers
- [ ] **Schedule Winterization** — complete before first frost
- [ ] Cost: $100–150; hire a professional if not comfortable doing it yourself

---

## Winter (November – February)

### ❄️ Top Priorities
- [ ] **November fertilizer** — the most important annual fertilizer
  - Winterizer product focused on Potassium (K) (e.g., 24-0-10 or 8-0-24)
  - Root strengthening and frost protection
- [ ] **Full sprinkler OFF** + winterization complete

> "My most important fertilizer is the one before winter."

### Mowing
- [ ] Mowing can stop once grass growth halts
- [ ] **Final mow: cut down to 2 inches** (prevents Snow Mold and matting)

### Monitoring
- [ ] Watch for **Snow Mold and fungus** → apply fungicide if needed
- [ ] **Fungicide can be used even during dormancy** (fertilizer has no effect during dormancy)
- [ ] Mark moss-affected areas → plan spring treatment

### Do NOT
- [ ] Apply fertilizer (no growth during dormancy → no effect)
- [ ] Overseed (temperature too low for germination)

## When to Call a Professional

- Recurring problems that won't resolve → run a soil test first
- Sprinkler installation or repair → professional irrigation contractor (e.g., RMA Irrigation)
- Lawn is 15+ years old with no overseeding → consider full replacement
- Tree trimming (especially specialty trees like Japanese Maple)

> "Grass has a lifespan just like we do of seven to ten years. If you're in a 15-year-old house and you never overseed it, that grass is dead."
