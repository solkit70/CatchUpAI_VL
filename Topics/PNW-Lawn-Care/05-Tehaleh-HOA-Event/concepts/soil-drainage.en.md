# Tehaleh Soil and Drainage Characteristics

## Core Problem: Glacial Till Soil

> "The soil here sucks. The builders put down like two inches of topsoil and slap sod on it and say, here you go, have fun."

As glaciers retreated from the Tehaleh area, they carried away the topsoil. The result:
- **Topsoil**: Only about 2 inches (5 cm)
- **Below**: Hard, compacted glacial till bedrock
- Builders installed sod over the bare minimum of topsoil

---

## Drainage Problems

### Causes
- The bedrock below cannot allow water to pass through
- Water cannot reach the natural water table
- Result: **Water pools near the surface** → waterlogging, root oxygen deprivation

### Symptoms
- Lawn flooding after heavy rain
- Chronically wet areas in specific zones
- Slow drainage in some areas even in summer

### Glacier Linden New Development Case
- Rock is exposed immediately when digging during construction
- Extreme example of glacial till conditions

---

## Drainage Improvement Methods

### 1. French Drain
- Gravel and perforated pipe buried underground to redirect water
- Already installed in some Tehaleh homes
- Important to design water flow direction so it doesn't run onto neighboring properties

### 2. Catch Basin
- Surface water inlet installed to collect rainwater and connect to storm drain
- Can be connected to existing stormwater system

### 3. Aeration
- Short-term solution: punch holes in topsoil to improve air and water penetration
- Not a permanent fix, but essential annual maintenance

---

## Impact on Lawn Care

| Problem | Impact | Response |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Shallow topsoil | Limited root growth, nutrient deficiency | Fertilize 6x/year, add organic matter |
| Poor drainage | Waterlogging, root oxygen deprivation, moss | Aeration, drainage systems |
| Hard subsoil | Roots can't go deep | Regular aeration |
| Low organic matter in topsoil | Poor self-sustaining nutrition | Apply compost |

---

## Soil pH Management

From slide: *"Tehaleh lawns thrive at pH 6.0–7.0. Too acidic = poor nutrient uptake. Fix with lime."*

- **Target pH**: 6.0–7.0
- Most Tehaleh soil: Tends to be too acidic
- **Treatment**: Apply lime to raise pH
- **Soil test**: Recommended every 2–3 years, approximately $100
  - Provides pH, nutrient levels, and recommended treatments
  - Local testing labs: Near Bellingham or Mount Vernon

---

## Practical Tips

- **Add compost** before planting grass (mix in 2–3 inches if possible)
- After aeration, apply **Topdressing** (spread thin layer of compost) to replenish organic matter
- For chronic drainage problems, hire a professional to install French Drain
- When soil improvement isn't working: **Run a soil test** first to identify the cause
