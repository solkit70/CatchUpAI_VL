# Expert Fertilizer Guide

## Annual Fertilizer Program

> "The live fertilizer for my clients is a six-time-a-year fertilizer."

Professional fertilizer schedule used by Neighbors Lawn Services for clients:

| Timing | Season | Key Nutrient | Purpose |
|--------|--------|--------------|---------|
| Early March | Spring start | Nitrogen (N) focused | Stimulate growth |
| April–May | Spring growth | Balanced | Build thickness |
| June | Early summer | Nitrogen | Prepare for summer |
| July–August | Midsummer | Nitrogen | Maintain color & thickness |
| September–October | Fall | Potassium (K) boost | Prepare for winter |
| November | **Pre-winter (most important)** | Potassium (K) focused | Root strengthening & frost protection |

> "My most important fertilizer is the one before [winter]."

---

## Understanding N-P-K

The three numbers on fertilizer packaging: **Nitrogen (N) – Phosphorus (P) – Potassium (K)**

### Nitrogen (N) — Spring & Summer Star
- **Role**: Leaf growth, green color
- **Deficiency symptom**: Yellow/gold grass
- **Response**: Choose fertilizer with higher nitrogen ratio
- Example: 30-0-4 (30% nitrogen, 0% phosphorus, 4% potassium)

### Phosphorus (P) — Root Development
- **Role**: Root development, new grass establishment
- **Tehaleh application**: Low priority for regular maintenance

### Potassium (K) — Key to Winter Survival
- **Role**: Cell wall strengthening, stress resistance, frost protection
- **Pre-winter**: Switch to fertilizer with higher potassium ratio
- **Recommended products**: 24-0-10 or 8-0-24

> "For Tehaleh's Country Green blend, avoid high-phosphorus fertilizers — the soil already has plenty."

⚠️ **Tehaleh Special Note**: Country Green blend lawn soil already has sufficient phosphorus (P) → **Avoid high-phosphorus (High-P) fertilizers**

---

## Fertilizer Forms

### Pellet Form Recommended
- Slow breakdown provides sustained nutrition
- Lower risk of over-application
- Easy to handle and store

### Caution with Iron (Fe)
> "I've cleaned out too many driveways with rust on it. Iron seeps into the concrete."

- Iron: Fast greening effect, short-term fix
- **Downside**: Red rust staining on driveways and sidewalks → concrete contamination
- Neighbors Lawn Services rarely uses iron-based fertilizers

---

## Fertilizer Application Timing

From slide: *"Apply in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation and maximize absorption."*

- **Best time**: Morning or evening (minimize evaporation, maximize absorption)
- **Summer**: Light feeding — apply small amounts to avoid burning stressed grass
- **Do not apply to dormant grass**: No growth means no absorption

---

## Post-Application Care

> "After you've watered it twice, two water-in cycles, it is fine."

- After applying fertilizer, water twice → absorbed into soil → safe
- Fertilizer remaining on leaf surface causes burn risk
- The hotter the temperature, the faster you need to water

---

## Tehaleh-Specific Strategy

- Thin topsoil means nutrients leach quickly → **Stick strictly to 6x/year schedule**
- Glacial till soil → low organic matter → **Combine with Compost** to boost fertilizer effectiveness
- Yellow grass → try **nitrogen fertilizer first** before iron
