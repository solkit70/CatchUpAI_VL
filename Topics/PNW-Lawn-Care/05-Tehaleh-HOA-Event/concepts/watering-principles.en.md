# Expert Watering Principles

## Core Principle: Deep, Infrequent, in the Morning

> "Water in the morning. Deep watering drives roots deeper."

### 1. Water in the Morning
- Maximize moisture absorption before daytime temperatures rise
- Evening watering → leaves stay wet overnight → **fungal and disease growth risk**
- Sprinkler recommendation: Set for 4–6 AM

### 2. Deep and Infrequent
- Goal: Water penetrates 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) deep
- **Frequent shallow watering** → roots stay near surface (vulnerable to drought)
- **Deep infrequent watering** → roots grow deeper seeking moisture (builds drought resistance)

### 3. Signs Your Lawn Needs Water
- Footprints remain (grass doesn't spring back)
- Color turns grayish or bluish
- Blades begin to curl

## Seasonal Watering Guide

| Season | Frequency | Weekly Goal | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Spring (Mar–May) | 2–3x/week | 1 inch | Adjust if rainy |
| Early Summer (June) | 3x/week | 1–1.5 inches | Seasonal Adjust 80% |
| Midsummer (Jul–Aug) | 4–5x/week | 1.5–2 inches | 100–110% |
| Fall (Sep–Oct) | 2x/week | 1 inch | Reduce to 80% |
| Winter (Nov–Feb) | Sprinkler OFF | — | Winterize to prevent freezing |

## Sprinkler System Notes

> "About 70% of Tehaleh homes have sprinkler systems at this point."

- Controllers like **Rain Bird ESP-ME3** in use
- Use Seasonal Adjust feature: automate by adjusting % each month
- Set to early morning: 4–6 AM recommended
- Check water pressure per zone: limit the number of zones running simultaneously

### Special Watering Rules After Overseeding
> "I'm watering three to five times a day. I'm sorry for your water bill this month, but it's just part of the process."

- During seed germination period, water **3–5 times per day**
- Surface must not dry out for more than 2 hours
- Duration: approximately 4–6 weeks until germination is complete

## Common Watering Mistakes

| Mistake | Result | Correct Approach |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Evening watering | Fungal & disease growth | Switch to morning watering |
| Daily shallow watering | Surface roots, drought vulnerability | Deep and infrequent |
| Auto-running during rain | Overwatering, waste | Install Rain Sensor |
| Same setting all summer | Under/overwatering | Monthly Seasonal Adjust |
