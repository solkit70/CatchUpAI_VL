# Tehaleh Grass Species Guide

## Tehaleh Community Standard Lawn

All residential lawns in Tehaleh are supplied by **Country Green Turf Farms (Olympia, WA)**. Every builder in the community used the same farm with the same blend.

> "If you go stand at the roundabout at the top of the hill as you come into the neighborhood, you'll see Country Green Turf Farms come in here all five weekdays a week."

---

## 3-Variety Blend

### 1. Kentucky Bluegrass
- **Role**: Provides thickness and deep green color
- **Germination**: 4–6 weeks (slowest)
- **Characteristics**: Long-lasting when well maintained; most important variety in the community
- **Care point**: Maintaining above 50% significantly improves durability

### 2. Perennial Ryegrass
- **Role**: Fast growth, used for patch repair
- **Germination**: Within 2 weeks (fast)
- **Characteristics**: Thin but fast-growing; used to fill bare areas
- **Application**: Primarily used for lawn repair and patch work

### 3. Fine Fescue
- **Role**: Provides drought resistance
- **Characteristics**: Key to survival during summer dry season (July–August)
- **Advantage**: Strong against PNW summer heat and dryness

---

## Lawn Installation Methods

### Sod — Most Residential Properties
- Grown for over a year at Country Green Farm, then cut and delivered
- **Mesh backing**: Contains plastic mesh for transport convenience
  - ⚠️ **Note**: Sod with mesh cannot go in regular recycling; must be disposed at landfill in a separate area
- The farm also supplies Tokyo Olympic Stadium and baseball parks

### Hydro-Seeding — Common Areas & New Developments
- Same blend applied as seed mixed with water and binder, then sprayed
- Used in newly developed common areas and Glacier Linden new development zones
- Lower cost than sod but takes more time to establish

---

## Tehaleh Soil and Lawn Relationship

- Glacial till soil → only about 2 inches of topsoil before hitting hard rock
- Builders installed sod over minimal topsoil → limited root growth space
- **Response**: Compensate with regular fertilization and aeration

---

| Item | Sod | Hydro-Seeding |
