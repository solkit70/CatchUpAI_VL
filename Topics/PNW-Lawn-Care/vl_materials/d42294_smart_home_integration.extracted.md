Amazon Alexa voice assistant you’ll need:
1. A Rain Bird WiFi-enabled controller with a LNK2 WiFi Module.
2. The latest Rain Bird App on your Apple iOS or Android smart phone or tablet.
3. An Amazon account with access to the Alexa companion app or Amazon
