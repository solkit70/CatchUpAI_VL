## Slide 2 — Grass Growing Essentials
**Your lawn needs four main things to grow:**

| 요소 | 설명 |
|------|------|
| ☀️ **Sun** | Photosynthesis is the engine of growth. Most turf grasses need 4–6 hours of direct sunlight. |
| 💧 **Water** | Essential for nutrient transport. Deep, infrequent watering encourages deep root systems. |
| 🌀 **Air** | Roots need oxygen to breathe. Relieving soil compaction is vital for air flow. |
| 🧪 **Nutrients** | Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium fuel lush, green growth and resilience. |

> **Sun + Water + Air + Nutrients + Good Soil = Healthy Grass**

## Slide 3 — Soil Health
**Your lawn is only as good as what's underneath**

- 🧪 **Soil Testing**
  Test your soil every 2–3 years. Reveals pH, nutrient levels, and what your lawn actually needs.

- ⚖️ **Ideal pH**
  Tehaleh lawns thrive at **pH 6.0–7.0**. Too acidic = poor nutrient uptake. Fix with lime.

*(IMG_4715)*

## Slide 4 — Improving Your Soil
**Simple steps for long-term lawn health**

- 🌍 **Topdressing**
  Apply a thin layer of compost in fall to improve soil structure and feed microbes.

- 🌀 **Compaction**
  Heavy foot traffic compacts soil. Aerate annually to restore oxygen and water flow.

> ℹ️ A simple soil test from your local extension office costs under $100 and can save you hundreds in wasted fertilizer.

## Slide 5 — Watering Your Tehaleh Grass
**Do this right, and everything gets easier**

| ❌ The Wrong Way | ✅ The Right Way |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Light watering every day** → shallow roots, weak grass, higher bills, more drought stress | **Deep watering less often** → stronger roots, tougher grass, lower bills, better drought resistance |

**The Formula:**
- 1 inch of water per week
- Water 2–3 times a week
- Run sprinklers until you collect 1 inch in a cup or rain gauge
- Water between **4–8 AM**

## Slide 7 — Why Too Much Water Hurts
**Overwatering is one of the most common mistakes**

- 🍄 **Fungal Diseases**
  Wet soil breeds brown patch, dollar spot & pythium. Grass stays damp too long, spreading fungus fast.

- 🐛 **Shallow Roots & Pests**
  Surface water = surface roots. Weak, shallow grass attracts grubs and chinch bugs.

- 🌿 **Weeds Spread Fast**
  Moist, thin spots give weed seeds the perfect place to germinate and take over.

> ☑️ **The fix: water deeply 2–3x per week, not a little every day.**

## Slide 10 — Mowing Your Tehaleh Blend
**Choosing Your Method: Bag vs. Mulch**

### Bagging — The preferred method for Tehaleh's unique climate.

**Why it matters:**
- Prevents **thatch buildup** (dead grass layer)
- Improves **airflow** & nutrient penetration
- Reduces **moss & disease** risk (PNW specific)
- Keeps lawn **clean & professional**

**Best for:**
- Weekly maintenance
- Spring/Fall cleanups
- Moisture-prone lawns

### Mulching — Leaves clippings on the lawn to break down.

**Pros:**
- Adds **nutrients** back to soil
- Saves time (no disposal)

**Tehaleh Risks:**
- Excessive **thatch buildup**
- Traps **extra moisture**
- Aggravates **moss problems**

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** In the PNW, bagging is generally recommended to manage moisture and prevent the moss issues common in Tehaleh conditions.

## Slide 11 — Mowing Best Practices
**Small habits that make a big difference**

| 원칙 | 내용 |
|------|------|
| ✂️ **The Golden 1/3 Rule** | Never cut more than one-third of the blade at once. Cutting too much stresses the grass and slows recovery. |
| 🔧 **Keep Blades Sharp** | Dull blades tear grass instead of cutting it cleanly, leaving ragged edges that invite disease. |
| 🔄 **Switch Directions** | Change your mowing pattern each week to prevent ruts and encourage upright growth. |

> ⚠️ Never mow wet grass — it clumps, clogs your mower, and spreads disease.

## Slide 13 — How to Choose the Right Fertilizer
**Understanding the N-P-K ratio**

| 성분 | 역할 |
|------|------|
| 🌿 **N – Nitrogen** | Drives lush, green leafy growth |
| 🌱 **P – Phosphorus** | Supports strong root development |
| 💪 **K – Potassium** | Builds disease & drought resistance |

| 시즌 | 제품 타입 |
|------|-----------|
| **Spring Formula** | High-N (e.g. **30-0-4**) — jumpstart green growth |
| **Fall / Winterizer** | High-K (e.g. **24-0-10 or 8-0-24**) — root strength & cold protection |

> ☑️ **For Tehaleh's Country Green blend, avoid high-phosphorus fertilizers — the soil already has plenty.**

## Slide 14 — Fertilizing Best Practices
**Get the most out of every application**

- 💧 **Always Water After**
  Water immediately after applying fertilizer to activate it and prevent burning.

- 🚫 **Never Apply to Wet Grass**
  Wet blades cause fertilizer to stick and burn. Wait for dry conditions.

> ☑️ When in doubt, less is more — over-fertilizing causes more damage than under-fertilizing.

*(IMG_4729)*

## Slide 15 — More Fertilizing Tips
**Timing and technique make all the difference**

- 🌡️ **Avoid Dormant Grass**
  Don't fertilize when grass is dormant or stressed — it can't absorb nutrients effectively.

- ⏰ **Timing Matters**
  Apply in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation and maximize absorption.

> ☑️ When in doubt, less is more — over-fertilizing causes more damage than under-fertilizing.

## Slide 17 — Dethatching
**Clear the way for a healthier lawn**

### Signs You Need It
- Lawn feels spongy underfoot
- Water pools instead of soaking in
- Fertilizer isn't working as well
- More pests or disease

### When & Why
- **Best time:** Late August – September

## Slide 18 — Overseeding
**Fill in the gaps and thicken your lawn**

1. **Best Time** — Late August–September for ideal germination.
2. **Mow Short First** — Cut grass to **< 2"** so seed reaches soil.
3. **Spread Seed** — Use Country Green blend at **3–4 lbs per 1,000 sq ft**.
4. **Water Daily** — Lightly water **twice daily** until sprouting (7–14 days).

## Slide 22 — Identifying & Fixing Common Issues
**Know the signs, know the solution**

| 문제 | 증상 | 처치 |
|------|------|------|
| 🐛 **Grubs** | Brown patches that lift like carpet. White C-shaped larvae under turf. | Beneficial nematodes or insecticide. |
| 🦟 **Chinch Bugs** | Yellow-brown patches in sunny spots. | Insecticidal soap or targeted spray. |
| 🦝 **Raccoons & Moles** | Torn turf or raised tunnels. | Eliminate grubs that attract them. |
| 💧 **Poor Drainage** | Pooling water, root damage, fungus risk. | Aerate or improve soil grading. |
| 🌿 **Excess Shade** | Thin, weak grass and mildew. | Trim branches or overseed with shade-tolerant mix. |

## Slide 24 — Professional Lawn Care: When to DIY vs Hire Help
**Know your limits and protect your lawn**

**Signs it may be time to call a pro:**
- Bare spots keep coming back after reseeding
- Strange color changes or fungus patches
- Pest damage spreads fast
- Watering problems leave some areas dry
- Soil is packed down or water does not drain well

**Why hire help:**
- **Hard or Repeating Problems** — Some issues keep coming back, spread fast, or need special tools. These can be hard to fix on your own.
- **Time, Tools, and Know-How** — Get help when the work takes too long, needs special skills, or uses tools you do not have.
