# Community Café – Home Lawn Care

**Date**: April 15, 2026
**Time**: 5:30 PM - 7:00 PM
**Location**: Discovery Park Community Center

## Event Description
Swing by the Discovery Park Community Center for our first Community Café of the year! These casual, informative sessions are designed to give homeowners helpful tips and insights on topics that matter most.

At this Café, we’ll be focusing on lawn care and grass health — perfect timing as we head into the warmer months here in Washington State. Learn how to prepare your yard for summer and get practical advice on how to keep your grass healthy, green, and thriving all season long.

Local landscape company, Neighbors Lawn Services, will be giving a presentation at 6 p.m. sharing expert insights and seasonal tips. Before and after the presentation, you’ll also have the opportunity to connect with both the Community Life Team and Neighbors Lawn Services to ask questions and get advice for your yard.
