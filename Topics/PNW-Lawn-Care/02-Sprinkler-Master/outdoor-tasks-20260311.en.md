### Step 0-B: Locate and Open Main Valve (Indoors + Outdoors, 10 min)

**What to find**: Sprinkler dedicated water supply valve (irrigation shutoff valve)

**Typical appearance**:
- Lever-type ball valve: Lever **parallel** to pipe = open, **perpendicular** = closed
- Handle-type gate valve: Turn **counterclockwise** all the way to open

**Procedure**:
1. [ ] Check garage → look near walls or floor where pipes run
2. [ ] If not found → look on exterior wall for backflow preventer
   - Usually at a low position on exterior wall, metal device with black/copper pipe connections
   - Photo: Take and save as `photos/backflow-preventer.jpg`
3. [ ] Check valve: Current state → [ ] Open / [ ] Closed
4. [ ] **If closed**: Open slowly — **turn 1/4 at a time, do not open all at once**
   - Reason: Sudden full opening can cause "water hammer" (hydraulic shock) and damage pipes
   - Turn slowly, listen for silence = normal
5. [ ] After fully opening, wait 30 seconds → listen for any abnormal sounds (thud, hiss, dripping)
6. [ ] Check for moisture on floor/walls near valve → none = normal

**Record**:
- Valve location: _________________________
- Valve type: _________________________
- Status after opening: [ ] Normal / [ ] Abnormal (___________________________)
