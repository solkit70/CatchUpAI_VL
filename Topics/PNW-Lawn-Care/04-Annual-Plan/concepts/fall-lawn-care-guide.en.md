# Fall Lawn Care Guide — PNW Zone 8b

**Created**: 2026-04-05
**Applies to**: Tehaleh, WA / Cool-season grass

---

## Why Fall Matters

> Fall is the second golden season for PNW lawn care, alongside spring.

After suffering from summer heat stress, Cool-season grass grows vigorously again in the cooler fall temperatures (10–15°C). This is when roots deepen and density recovers.

---

## Fall's Three Core Tasks

### 1. Overseeding

**Timing**: Early September – early October (soil temperature 15–20°C)
**Purpose**: Recover lawn density thinned by summer, suppress weeds

**Procedure**:
1. Dethatch first (seeds must contact the soil to germinate)
2. Choose seed: PNW Perennial Ryegrass + Tall Fescue mix
3. Seeding rate: 3–4 lb per 1,000 sq ft of existing lawn
4. Apply light topping after spreading (cover seeds)
5. For 2–3 weeks before germination: Light daily watering (prevent seeds from drying out)
6. **Note**: Only possible after pre-emergent effect expires (August and later)

**My Lawn**: Overseeding possible after early August 2026 (Scotts Halts pre-emergent expires)

---

### 2. Fall Fertilizer

**Timing**: September–early October
**Purpose**: Strengthen roots, prepare for winter

**Choose products high in Potassium (K)**

| Spring Fertilizer | Fall Fertilizer |
|------------------|-----------------|
| High Nitrogen (N) (promotes growth) | High Potassium (K) (strengthens roots) |
| Example: 30-0-4 | Example: Scotts Turf Builder WinterGuard |
| Green leaf growth | Root energy storage |

**Caution**: No nitrogen fertilizer in late fall (after mid-October) → risk of frost damage

---

### 3. Dethatching

**Timing**: September–October
**Purpose**: Remove thatch before winter to improve aeration and drainage

**Threshold**: Needed when thatch exceeds 1.25 cm

**Method**:
- Use a rented Power Dethatcher (Vertical Mower)
- Collect all debris generated by the work
- Proceed directly to Overseeding (optimal seed-soil contact with exposed soil)

---

## Recommended Fall Task Order

```
Early September: Confirm soil temperature (start when below 20°C)
    ↓
Dethatching (aeration + thatch removal)
    ↓
Overseeding (seed application)
    ↓
Fall fertilizer application (high potassium product)
    ↓
Germination period: Light daily watering (2–3 weeks)
    ↓
Late October–early November: Winterizing
```
