## Key Lessons from This Module

### Lesson 1: Think in annual cycles, not one-time tasks
Great spring work doesn't mean you're done. Map out the full year — spring → summer → fall → winterizing — on a calendar so you never miss a critical window.

### Lesson 2: Fall is just as important as spring
September Overseeding, October fall fertilizer, November winterizing — fall tasks determine how well your lawn looks next spring. Overseeding must happen in fall because Pre-emergent used in spring blocks it.

### Lesson 3: Always winterize your sprinklers
PNW is mild, but Tehaleh's elevation means frost is real. Water left in pipes can freeze and crack. Complete a Blow-out or gravity drain before the first hard freeze.
