## Winterizing Procedure (Blow Out Method)

### Pre-Preparation

- [ ] Prepare compressor (20–25 CFM or higher recommended) — or book professional service
- [ ] Wear safety glasses (compressed air hazard)
- [ ] Confirm controller location

### Step 1 — Controller Settings

- [ ] ESP-ME3 dial → **Manual Watering** position
- [ ] Start Station: Set to Station 1
- [ ] Confirm all programs are deactivated

### Step 2 — Shut Off Main Water

- [ ] Close main supply valve (before backflow preventer)
- [ ] Confirm no leaks after closing

### Step 3 — Blow Out Execution

- [ ] Connect compressor to Blow Out port on backflow preventer
- [ ] **Manually run Station 1** → use compressed air to push water out of pipes
- [ ] Continue until only air comes out (approx. 2–3 min per station)
- [ ] Repeat for Stations 2–8 in order
- [ ] **Caution**: One station at a time; do not exceed pressure (risk of head damage)

### Step 4 — Finish Up

- [ ] Disconnect compressor
- [ ] Turn both backflow preventer valves to 45° closed (not completely closed — leave room for pipe expansion)
- [ ] Wrap backflow preventer with insulation (optional — for extreme cold protection)
- [ ] ESP-ME3 dial → **OFF**
- [ ] Seasonal Adjust → **0%** (secondary safety measure)

### Step 5 — Record

- [ ] Record Winterizing completion date: ___________
- [ ] Record any notable findings (leaks, broken heads, etc.): ___________

## Spring Startup (Re-activation) Checklist

### Timing

| Basis | Date |
|-------|------|
| Last frost risk | Mid-March |
| **Recommended Startup Window** | **Mid-March – early April** |

### Procedure

**Step 1 — Visual Inspection**
- [ ] Check pipes and heads externally (any winter damage)
- [ ] Check backflow preventer condition

**Step 2 — Open Main Valve Slowly**
- [ ] Open main valve before backflow preventer **1/4 at a time slowly**
- [ ] Opening too fast risks Water Hammer (hydraulic shock) and pipe damage
- [ ] Takes approx. 30 seconds to open completely

**Step 3 — Controller Restart**
- [ ] ESP-ME3 dial → **Date/Time** → Verify and correct date/time
- [ ] Seasonal Adjust → **70%** (early spring initial value)
- [ ] Dial → Keep at **OFF** (do not switch to AUTO yet)

**Step 4 — Manual Test per Station**
- [ ] Dial → **Manual Watering**
- [ ] Run each station starting from Station 1 for 2 minutes
- [ ] Confirm each station operates normally
- [ ] Record broken heads, leak points

**Step 5 — Repair Issues Then Restore Schedule**
- [ ] Replace broken heads if needed
- [ ] Re-verify Program A/B/C settings
- [ ] Verify Water Days
- [ ] Early May: Dial → **AUTO** switch
