## Pre-emergent vs. Post-emergent Difference

| Type | When It Acts | Target | Example Products |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Pre-emergent** | Before seed germination | Annual weed seeds (Crabgrass, etc.) | Scotts Halts, Preen |
| **Post-emergent** | After weeds are growing | Already established weeds | Ortho Weed B Gon, Roundup |

> **Important**: Pre-emergent does not kill weeds — it **prevents germination**. Use post-emergent for weeds that are already growing.

## PNW Key Weed Identification Guide

| Weed | Type | Characteristics | Season | Control Method |
|------|------|----------------|--------|---------------|
| **Crabgrass** | Annual (summer) | Wide blades, spreads finger-like | May–Aug | Pre-emergent ✅ (done) |
| **Dandelion** | Perennial broadleaf | Deep taproot, yellow flower, seeds | Spring/Fall | Post-emergent (selective) |
| **White Clover** | Perennial broadleaf | 3 leaflets, white flower, nitrogen-fixing | Spring–Summer | Post-emergent + density improvement |
| **Creeping Buttercup** | Perennial broadleaf | Yellow flower, creeping stems | Spring–Summer | Post-emergent |
| **Annual Bluegrass (Poa annua)** | Annual | Light green, germinates in early spring | Fall–Spring | Pre-emergent ✅ (done) |
| **Moss** | — | Shaded/wet areas, green mat | Spring/Fall | pH correction + drainage improvement |
| **Creeping Bentgrass** | Perennial grass-type | Fine blades, spreads mat-like | Year-round | Non-selective herbicide then Overseeding |

## Herbicide Usage Precautions

### Restrictions After Pre-emergent Application (Currently Applies)

- ❌ **No Overseeding** — until early August 2026
- ✅ Mowing allowed (2–3 days after application)
- ✅ Post-emergent use allowed (to treat existing weeds)
- ❌ No repeat pre-emergent application

### Selective vs. Non-Selective Herbicides

| Type | Characteristics | Usage Caution |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **Selective** (Weed B Gon, etc.) | Removes broadleaf weeds only, safe for lawn | Can be applied directly to lawn |
| **Non-selective** (Roundup, etc.) | Removes all plants | Will kill grass if it contacts — spot treatment only |

---

## The Root Solution to Weed Prevention

> **The best weed preventer is a healthy, dense lawn**

- High lawn density means weed seeds have no opportunity to reach the soil
- Fall Overseeding → density improvement → weed suppression positive cycle
- Proper watering + fertilizing → maintains lawn's competitive edge

**My Lawn 2026 Goal**: Overseeding (after August) → minimize weed occurrence next spring
