## Key Lessons from This Module

### Lesson 1: Brown lawn ≠ dead lawn
During PNW dry summers (July–August), cool-season grasses go **dormant** — they turn brown, but the roots are still alive. Natural recovery happens when fall rains return.

### Lesson 2: Diagnose first, treat second
Lawn problems vary widely by cause (disease, pests, nutrient deficiency, water stress). Applying fertilizer, water, or pesticide without knowing the cause often makes things worse.

### Lesson 3: Summer is about schedule optimization
After Pre-emergent is applied, summer success comes down to irrigation scheduling and weed management. Adjust the ESP-ME3 Seasonal Adjust monthly to avoid over- or under-watering.
