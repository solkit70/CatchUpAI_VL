## Summer Irrigation Formula

**Goal**: 1–1.5 inches per week (25–38 mm)

| Head Type | Precipitation Rate | Run Time to Achieve 1 inch/week |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| Rotary Head (Rotor) | 0.5 in/hour | 120 min/week |
| Fixed Head (Spray) | 1.5 in/hour | 40 min/week |

**My Lawn Estimate** (assuming rotary heads):
- Current 10 min/station × 5x/week = 50 min → approx. 0.42 in/week
- To reach summer goal: Run Time 20 min × 4x/week = 80 min → approx. 0.67 in/week + Seasonal Adjust 150%

## Lawn Stress Signals — Field Check

| Signal | Meaning | Immediate Response |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| Footprints remain for 15+ min | Moisture deficiency | Manual Run |
| Blades curling or folding | Drought stress | Increase watering amount |
| Blue-green → blue-gray color change | Severe drought | Water immediately |
| No dew in early morning | Watering needed | Check schedule |
