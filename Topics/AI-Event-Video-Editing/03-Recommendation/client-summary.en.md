## Methods to Try First
[한국어](client-summary.md)

**The goal is to pick the needed scenes from long event footage, make a 3-minute video, and finish it in a familiar editing program.** Below is a tentative order based on research of documentation. It has not been tested with real video, and we do not yet know the version you use, your budget, or whether outside upload is allowed.

| Order | What to try | Free · paid conditions | Points to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Premiere you use now | Find remarks and scenes and edit | Paid subscription needed. Transcription is included in the subscription; check access to trial-version features | Search and automatic editing of a finished video are different. The feature that makes a first edit through conversation is a trial version. |
| 2. CapCut you use now | Check automatic highlights and captions | Basic editing is free. The AI features you want · output conditions need checking | See whether it is easy to make one landscape 3-minute video in your version. |
| 3. If not enough, compare Vidio | Check scene selection with a short video that can be made public | Free preview available. Delivery-quality output · passing edit information needs credits | Captions · transition effects may need to be redone in Premiere. |

Basis: research cards for [Premiere](../02-Methods-Research/cards/premiere-native-ai.en.md) · [CapCut](../02-Methods-Research/cards/capcut-ai.en.md) · [Vidio](../02-Methods-Research/cards/vidio-vibeedit.en.md). The order is a suggestion to first find work that can be reduced in familiar tools.

## Cost and Intro

Vidio's general AI editing needs **108 credits per 1 hour of source**, but **the amount included in Pro 20 at $10 billed monthly is 20 credits**. So the amount included in this plan alone is not enough to process 1 hour of source, and even if the finished video is 3 minutes, cost should be judged by the source length. This is based on USD · monthly billing checked on 2026-10-24, and the final charge is separate. [Cost · terms check record](../02-Methods-Research/review-followup.en.md)

We check the free preview separately from the quality and watermark-free output needed for actual delivery. OpusClip is also an alternative, but the difference between its free trial and paid features needs to be checked.

For the intro, we first see whether existing After Effects · CapCut templates can be used. If you need a template where the event name · date · logo are changed every time, Changsoo Park can compare it with the Remotion approach, but help with making it and the terms of use need to be checked.
