## KakaoTalk Draft

I put together a draft guide comparing ways to edit event videos with AI. Please look at the one-page summary first, and start by checking which tasks can be reduced in the Premiere and CapCut you already use. If needed, Vidio can also be compared using a short copy that is allowed to be uploaded.

When I tested it myself, the step of fixing the speech connections and length after the automatic draft was important. In Vidio, the edit explanation and the actual length differed, and the result improved after manual correction. I also made a generative intro, but the template comparison and the Premiere hand-off still need to be checked.

If you let me know the versions and subscriptions of Premiere and CapCut you use, an example of the finished video you want, and whether the test video can be uploaded externally, it will help decide the scope of the next test. We can look at pricing and paid features together while checking them in the actual account.
