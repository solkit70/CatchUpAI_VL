# Confirmation Questions for the Client
[한국어](client-questions.md)

> We ask these to check that the requirements card is correct and to fit the manual to the client's environment. **Ask everything at once in M1** — so there is no need to ask again after M2. You can **send the message below as is** (KakaoTalk · email). When the answers come in, update the "To confirm" cells and "7. What I can and cannot do" in [requirements-card.en.md](requirements-card.en.md).

## Message to Send

```
Hello, this is Changsoo Park.
I have started researching "editing event videos with AI," as you mentioned. Before anything else, I would like to check whether I understood correctly, explain what I can do for you, and ask a few questions.

■ What I understood
I understand that you want to tell AI "use these kinds of scenes" for event video shot over several hours, have it pick only the matching parts, edit them into a video of about 3 minutes, and add an opening intro and effects in the middle. I also heard that you mainly use Premiere Pro, also use After Effects and CapCut, and that paid services are fine if they help your work.

■ What I can and cannot do for you
- I will research several methods (Claude · ChatGPT connection tools, dedicated AI video editing tools, Premiere's own AI features, generative AI, etc.), put them in a comparison table, and make an installation and usage manual for the recommended method.
- For tools that can be used for free, I will try them myself with public event videos and include screen captures and the places where I got stuck in the manual.
- I cannot use paid services myself (including Premiere and After Effects, and trials that require registering a card). For these tools, I will make the manual from official documentation and tutorials and mark them "not tried myself." Instead, when you use them, I can guide you by meeting in person and sitting next to you, or by watching together through screen sharing.

■ Working style (choose whichever is comfortable; mixing is fine)
- A) I research and give you the results and the manual
- B) We meet and research · try things together on my computer (paid tools are tested with your account)
- C) I install the AI learning tool I use (VibeLearn AI) on your computer and show you how to use it, and then you research directly in the direction you want (I will help when you get stuck. A paid Claude subscription is needed)

■ What I would like to ask (you only need to answer what you know)

[Work environment]
1. Is your computer Windows or Mac?
2. Which Adobe plan do you use, and which version of Premiere? (Premiere only / full Creative Cloud)
3. Do you use Claude or ChatGPT on a paid plan?
4. Have you ever installed a plugin or extension in a program yourself?

[Footage and finished videos]
5. How many hours of footage do you usually have, and with what camera and at what resolution (e.g. 4K, 1080p) do you shoot? Do you use several cameras?
6. Is the sound of speeches and presentations recorded clearly with a microphone? (This matters when AI turns speech into text to find scenes)
7. In what form do you deliver the finished video? (landscape 16:9 / vertical 9:16 for social media / both, and the length)
8. Could you share a link to one 3-minute video you made recently? I will use it as the standard for a "good result."
9. How many hours does it usually take you to make one 3-minute video now, and about how many event videos do you make per month?

[What you want from AI]
10. What do you most want AI to do? Please also tell me what takes the most time.
   - A) Picking usable scenes from a long video
   - B) Joining the chosen scenes into 3 minutes
   - C) Adding an intro, transition effects, and captions
11. By "Sidens," did you mean ByteDance's Seedance (an AI that makes new video from text)? I would like to know whether you were thinking of it for editing your footage, or for making new scenes such as an intro. If you can tell me where you saw it, I will look it up.
12. For intros and effects, do you use the same template (logo, event name caption) for every event? Do you already have After Effects templates or a music source you use?
13. Do you want to give instructions to AI in Korean? Do you also need Korean captions?

[Cost and video handling]
14. About how much can you spend per month? (A rough range is fine)
15. Is it okay to upload event videos to outside AI servers? Are there cases where the event host would not want that?
16. Could you give me part of a past event's footage (20–30 minutes) for testing? Only if the event host agrees; I will use it only for testing and will not make it public anywhere. If that is difficult, I will test with my own public videos.

[Manual]
17. What form of manual is most convenient for you? (document · PDF / screen recording video / a time to go through it together) By when do you need it?
18. Of the working styles A), B), and C) above, which is most comfortable for you? If B) or C), please also tell me which days and times are good to meet.

Once I have your answers, I will pick the method that fits and make a usage manual for it.
```

## Reason for Each Question

| # | Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PC (Windows/Mac) | Manual screens · installation steps differ |
| 2 | Adobe plan · Premiere version | Scope of Premiere's own AI features, XML import support (some services need Premiere 2022 or later) |
| 3 | Paid Claude · ChatGPT subscription | Whether category A (connection tools · MCP) can be used at no extra cost |
| 4 | Experience installing plugins | Whether the MCP · extension (CEP panel) approach fits the client |
| 5 | Footage length · gear · resolution · number of cameras | Upload limits · processing cost · multi-camera support |
| 6 | Recording quality | Accuracy of scene finding based on transcription (speech → text) |
| 7 | Delivery format | Tools for landscape highlights and tools for vertical short-form are different |
| 8 | Example of a finished video | Standard for a "good 3-minute video" · M4 evaluation sheet |
| 9 | Current time taken · monthly count | Basis for comparing time saved against monthly cost |
| 10 | Must-have features · most time-consuming work | Priorities for the recommendation |
| 11 | Meaning · source of "Sidens" | Distinguish generative (new video) from editing-based (editing footage) |
| 12 | Intro template · templates · music | Whether to do finishing with AI or whether reusing existing templates is enough · music copyright |
| 13 | Korean | Narrow down to tools that support Korean instructions · captions |
| 14 | Monthly budget | Range of the recommendation |
| 15 | Uploading to outside servers | Whether cloud services can be used |
| 16 | Test footage | Whether free tools can be tested with real event video — without consent, use Changsoo Park's public videos |
| 17 | Manual format · deadline | Format and schedule of the M6 deliverable |
| 18 | Working style (A · B · C) | How to fill the gap of paid-tool trials Changsoo Park cannot do — options A · B · C in section 8 of the requirements card. B · C are done in person |
