## What This Module Found

- The exact product the client meant by "Sidens" (시덴스) is unverified. **Correction on 10/24**: Seedance official documentation also describes reference editing, and this is different from highlight editing of hours-long event footage. → [M2 correction basis](../02-Methods-Research/cards/seedance.en.md)
- There are several community **connection tools (MCP) that let Claude operate Premiere directly**. However, they are not official Adobe tools and are hard to install.
- Some services (Vidio.ai · OpusClip · VEA, etc.) promote a **long video → prompt → highlight → Premiere XML** flow — since the client is a Premiere user, this connection is the key point.
- **Changsoo Park cannot try paid services himself** (including card-required trials). The manual marks "what I tried myself" separately from "what I organized from official documentation."
- Changsoo Park lives close to the client, so **we can meet in person**. The working style will be chosen with the client from three options — A. Changsoo Park researches and delivers a manual · B. meet and work together on Changsoo Park's PC · C. install VibeLearn AI on the client's PC, give guidance, and then the client researches on their own (section 8 of the requirements card).
