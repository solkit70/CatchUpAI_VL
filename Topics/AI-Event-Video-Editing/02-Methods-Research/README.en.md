I compared features, cost, limits, Korean support and Premiere handoff using official documentation and material written by community authors. ## What changed in my judgment in this research

The [official Premiere Assistant](cards/premiere-native-ai.en.md#official-ai-assistant-public-beta) and the [community MCP](cards/premiere-mcp.en.md#summary) are different paths. [Seedance](cards/seedance.en.md#features-and-correction-of-the-earlier-description) also supports reference editing, so I corrected the earlier flat statement that "it cannot edit." It is still kept separate from highlight editing of hours-long recorded footage.

For external editing services, the [elements missing from XML](cards/vidio-vibeedit.en.md#limits-of-the-premiere-handoff), the [free trial limits](cards/opusclip-clipanything.en.md#pricing-free-trial-processing-volume) and the [output length that cannot be set directly](cards/memories-vea.en.md#features-and-the-boundaries-between-different-docs) are the points to check for real adoption. Code tools are not candidates that solve scene selection automatically. They are things to compare by splitting up their roles.
