# US FDE vs Global AX Context

## Key Distinction

FDE and AX are not the same concept. FDE usually refers to a customer-facing technical role. AX, or AI Transformation, usually refers to an organizational transformation agenda. They overlap in enterprise AI adoption, but they operate differently in the job market.

## Regional Comparison

| Region | Main Language | Unit of Action | Technical Role Position | Career Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | FDE, FDSWE, Applied AI Engineer, Solutions Engineer | Role/title, product team, GTM team | Builder and deployment owner close to customer | Match evidence to job posting verbs and archetype |
| Korea | AX, AI transformation, AI internalization | Enterprise transformation project, SI, consulting | Use case, PoC, implementation, change management | Start from AX consultant, solution specialist, implementation lead |
| Japan | DX to AX, continuous transformation | Enterprise analytics and operating model | AI capability added to existing DX programs | Combine Japanese enterprise context, process documentation, AI literacy |
| Europe | Enterprise AI, AI governance, regulated AI | Compliance-aware deployment program | Technical delivery under privacy, risk, and governance constraints | Emphasize data governance, compliance, security-aware AI delivery |

## US FDE Context

US FDE is more role/title centered. Postings often directly mention customer discovery, prototype, integration, deployment, and product feedback. Candidates must show technical artifacts and adoption evidence.

## Korea AX Context

Korea AX is often discussed as enterprise transformation. Work may be performed by SI firms, consulting teams, internal DX/DT organizations, or business innovation teams. The title may not be FDE even if some tasks are FDE-like.

## Japan DX-to-AX Context

Japan's DX-to-AX framing often extends existing digital transformation into AI-driven transformation. Manufacturing, energy, finance, and large enterprises may value process documentation, stakeholder alignment, and data governance.

## European Regulated AI Context

Europe places strong emphasis on privacy, governance, risk, and sector regulation. FDE-like roles may need to handle auditability, human oversight, data boundaries, and vendor risk from the beginning.

## Career Translation Rule
