**Status**: Complete  
**Estimated study time**: 4h  
**Module goal**: Define a realistic path for students and junior candidates preparing for FDE or FDE-adjacent roles.

2. [guides/six-month-plan.en.md](guides/six-month-plan.en.md)

3. [examples/portfolio-project-briefs.en.md](examples/portfolio-project-briefs.en.md)

## Core Takeaway

Junior candidates rarely have direct FDE evidence. They need to build proof through projects that show customer problem framing, technical implementation, eval thinking, and adoption metrics.
