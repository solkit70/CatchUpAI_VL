# 90-Day Senior Transition Plan

## Days 1-30: Inventory and Reframe

### Goals

- Identify prior projects that resemble FDE work.
- Translate experience into customer problem, technical action, production impact, and reusable learning.
- Identify AI/LLM production gaps.

### Outputs

- experience inventory
- 5 rewritten resume bullets
- target FDE archetype selection
- AI gap checklist

## Days 31-60: Build AI Evidence

### Goals

- Build one AI workflow demo connected to your domain or prior enterprise experience.
- Add evals, security boundary, and rollout thinking.
- Produce a clear architecture diagram and README.

### Outputs

- working prototype
- eval dataset
- architecture diagram
- 5-minute demo script

## Days 61-90: Package and Apply

### Goals

- Create an application package.
- Practice customer scenario and system design interviews.
- Apply to FDE and FDE-adjacent roles.

### Outputs

- one-page FDE narrative
- portfolio README
- resume bullets
- target company matrix
- mock interview answers

## Weekly Cadence

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Experience inventory and role analysis |
| 3-4 | Resume narrative and target company map |
| 5-6 | AI prototype build |
| 7-8 | Eval, security, and rollout additions |
| 9-10 | Demo script and portfolio polish |
| 11-12 | Applications and interview practice |

## Success Criteria

- You can explain why your prior experience is FDE-relevant.
- You can show at least one AI workflow artifact.
- You can discuss evals, security, and adoption.
- You can answer customer scenario questions without jumping too quickly to solution.
