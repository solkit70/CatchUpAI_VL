**Status**: Complete  
**Estimated study time**: 3h

## Learning Order

2. [examples/original-vs-ai-fde-comparison.en.md](examples/original-vs-ai-fde-comparison.en.md)  
   Compares the original Palantir-style model with AI-era FDE roles at companies such as OpenAI, Cursor, Scale AI, and Hebbia.

## Core Takeaway

The Palantir model made FDE known as a role that sits close to real operations, not just product development. Modern AI FDE roles keep the embedded delivery pattern but apply it to AI models, developer tools, data infrastructure, agents, evals, and production adoption.
