# Role Title Reading Guide

## Why Titles Are Misleading

AI companies use titles inconsistently. "Forward Deployed Engineer" may mean deep engineering at one company and technical GTM at another. "Applied AI Engineer" may be internal product engineering in one posting and customer-facing deployment in another.

The safest method is to read the posting by work pattern.

## Five Things to Extract

| Item | What to Look For | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Verbs | build, deploy, integrate, evaluate, debug, partner, unblock | Shows actual work |
| Customer | enterprise, public sector, developers, domain experts | Shows context |
| Technical Surface | API, backend, data, evals, security, agents | Shows depth |
| Output | prototype, integration, demo, architecture, rollout | Shows responsibility |
| Success Metric | adoption, production use, revenue, customer unblock | Shows accountability |

## FDE-Like Signal Checklist

- [ ] Customer problem discovery is explicitly mentioned.
- [ ] The role builds or modifies technical systems.
- [ ] Integration with customer systems is expected.
- [ ] Production, deployment, or rollout is mentioned.
- [ ] Evals, observability, security, or reliability appear.
- [ ] The role feeds customer learnings back into product.
- [ ] The role requires comfort with ambiguity.

If most boxes are checked, the role is FDE-like even if the title is different.

## Common Title Translations

| Title | Could Mean | What to Verify |
|---|---|---|
| Forward Deployed Engineer | Core FDE, field engineering, technical GTM | How much coding and deployment ownership? |
| Field Engineer | Customer-facing deployment or support | Is it implementation or mainly troubleshooting? |
| Applied AI Engineer | AI app builder or customer deployment engineer | Is customer context central? |
| Solutions Engineer | Pre-sales technical validation | Does it continue after sale? |
| AI Adoption Engineer | Enablement and workflow transformation | Is there hands-on implementation? |
| Deployment Manager | Rollout coordination | Is the role technical or project management? |

## Reading Example

If a posting says:

- "partner with strategic customers"
- "build prototypes"
- "integrate our APIs into customer workflows"
- "debug model behavior"
- "drive production adoption"
- "bring product feedback to engineering"

Then it is strongly FDE-like.

If a posting says:

- "deliver product demos"
- "support account executives"
- "prepare technical presentations"
- "respond to RFPs"

Then it is closer to solutions engineering or sales engineering.

## Candidate Action

For every target job, create a one-page extraction sheet:

```text
Role title:
Company:
Customer type:
Repeated verbs:
Technical stack:
Customer-facing depth:
Deployment responsibility:
FDE-likeness score:
My evidence:
My gap:
```

This prevents applying based on title alone.
