## What this is

An FDE is **an engineer deployed close to the customer, who makes their company's technology actually work inside the customer's real workflows.** An AI demo takes a day to build; putting it into enterprise operations surfaces data permissions, security, evaluation, cost, integration and governance all at once. The FDE closes that gap in the field — which is why OpenAI, Anthropic, Scale AI and Cursor are hiring for it so aggressively right now.

This pack walks through the role in order: **definition → history → company archetypes → technical stack → job market → preparation paths by background → portfolio.** The primary focus is the US market, with comparisons to Korea's AX, Japan's DX-to-AX, and Europe's regulated-AI contexts.

### Who it's for

- Anyone interested in emerging AI-era careers
- People who have heard of FDE but want to know what it actually is
- People actively targeting an FDE or FDE-adjacent role
- Junior / senior IT / non-IT — **each background has its own preparation track**
