# Remotion Series Outline

## Scope

This is a pre-production document for Remotion video creation. It provides the educational structure only. Actual Remotion implementation, assets, subtitles, audio, and rendering are out of scope.

## Series Concept

**Series title**: Forward Deployed Engineer: The Customer-Embedded Engineer of the AI Era  
**Audience**: students, software engineers, senior IT professionals, non-IT domain experts, and people studying the US AI job market  
**Format**: six episodes, 5-8 minutes each  
**Tone**: practical, analytical, career-focused

## Episode Plan

| EP | Title | Core Question | Source Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What Is an FDE? | Is FDE engineering, consulting, or sales? | M1, M2 |
