# Episode Scripts

## EP1 - What Is an FDE?

### Hook

"In the AI era, companies do not need impressive demos only. They need systems that actually work inside real business workflows. That is where the Forward Deployed Engineer comes in."

### Flow

1. Define FDE as a customer-facing engineer.
2. Distinguish FDE from SWE, consultant, and solutions engineer.
3. Explain production adoption.
4. Explain field signal and product feedback.

### Closing

"An FDE makes technology work in the customer's real environment."

## EP2 - From Palantir to AI FDE

### Hook

"FDE became famous through Palantir, but the pattern is now reappearing across AI companies."

### Flow

1. Explain Palantir-style embedded delivery.
2. Show what stayed the same: operational workflow and reusable patterns.
3. Show what changed: LLMs, RAG, agents, evals, and AI adoption.
4. Connect the adoption gap to modern AI companies.

### Closing

"AI FDE is the forward deployed model applied to AI adoption."

## EP3 - Company Archetypes

### Hook

"The same FDE title can mean very different work depending on the company."

### Flow

1. Explain AI Lab FDE.
2. Explain Developer Tool FDE.
3. Explain Data/Agent Infrastructure FDE.
4. Explain Vertical AI FDE.
5. Tell candidates to read verbs, not only titles.

### Closing

"A strong strategy starts with choosing the right FDE archetype."

## EP4 - AI FDE Tech Stack

### Hook

"An AI FDE does not need to master every layer, but must understand the trade-offs that make AI usable in production."

### Flow

1. Walk through discovery, scoping, prototype, eval, integration, rollout, handoff.
2. Explain RAG, agents, evals, observability, and security.
3. Show why production AI requires more than prompting.

### Closing

"The AI FDE stack is a decision system for production adoption."

## EP5 - Entry Paths by Background

### Hook

"FDE preparation looks different for a junior, a senior IT professional, and a non-IT domain expert."

### Flow

1. Juniors need portfolio proof.
2. Senior IT professionals need to translate delivery experience.
3. Non-IT candidates often need adjacent paths first.
4. Global contexts such as Korea AX, Japan DX-to-AX, and European regulated AI should not be confused with US FDE.

### Closing

"There is no single FDE path. Each background must build the right evidence."

## EP6 - Portfolio and Interview Prep

### Hook

"An FDE portfolio is not just a GitHub repo. It is proof that you can turn a customer problem into a deployable AI workflow."

### Flow

1. Start with customer scenario.
2. Show architecture and data flow.
3. Add evals, security, rollout, and metrics.
4. Explain interview loop preparation.
5. Close with final candidate package.

### Closing

"FDE candidates must prove adoption, not just implementation."
