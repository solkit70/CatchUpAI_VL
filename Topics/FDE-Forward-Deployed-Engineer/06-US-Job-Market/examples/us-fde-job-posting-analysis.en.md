# US FDE Job Posting Analysis

## Common Capability Themes

| Rank | Capability | What It Means |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Customer-facing engineering | Ability to work directly with customers while making technical decisions |
| 2 | Ambiguity tolerance | Comfort scoping incomplete and changing requirements |
| 3 | Hands-on building | Ability to prototype, integrate, debug, and ship |
| 4 | AI workflow understanding | LLMs, RAG, agents, evals, model behavior, AI product adoption |
| 5 | Data and integration | APIs, connectors, ETL, schemas, permissions |
| 6 | Production mindset | Reliability, security, observability, handoff |
| 7 | Communication | Ability to explain trade-offs to engineers, executives, and users |
| 8 | Domain or public sector context | Especially important in regulated or government roles |
| 9 | Travel or onsite readiness | Some roles require customer proximity |
| 10 | Product feedback | Ability to generalize field learnings into product direction |

## Posting Extraction Fields

For each job posting, extract:

- company
- role title
- location
- years of experience
- technical stack
- customer type
- travel or onsite expectation
- security clearance or compliance requirement
- compensation range
- repeated verbs
- implied interview topics

## Interpreting Years of Experience

When a posting asks for 5+ years, it usually does not mean only time spent coding. For FDE roles, it often means the candidate has seen enough real-world complexity to handle customers, ambiguous scope, trade-offs, production constraints, and stakeholder pressure.

## Public Sector vs Commercial FDE

| Dimension | Public Sector / Gov | Commercial Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Security | Clearance or strict controls may matter | Enterprise security review and vendor risk matter |
| Sales Cycle | Longer and more regulated | Faster but still complex |
| Deployment | Compliance-heavy, often onsite or hybrid | Varies by customer and product |
| Success Metric | Mission outcome, reliability, risk control | Adoption, productivity, revenue expansion |
| Candidate Signal | Public sector, security, regulated delivery | Enterprise SaaS, AI workflow, customer adoption |

## Strong Candidate Evidence

Strong evidence includes:

- shipped customer-facing technical projects
- worked with messy customer requirements
- built AI workflow prototype with evals
- integrated APIs or internal systems
- handled security or data boundaries
- created rollout or adoption plan
- translated field feedback into product improvements

## Weak Candidate Signals

Weak evidence includes:

- only coursework with no working artifact
- only prompt usage without system design
- only consulting slides without implementation
- only internal engineering with no customer context
- only demos with no adoption or eval plan

## Summary

US FDE job postings reward proof. The strongest candidates show that they can enter a customer environment, build under uncertainty, and make AI or software systems work in production-like workflows.
