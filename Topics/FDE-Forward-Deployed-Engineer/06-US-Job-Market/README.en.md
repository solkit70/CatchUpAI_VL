# M6 - US Job Posting Based Capability Analysis

**Status**: Complete  
**Estimated study time**: 5h  
**Module goal**: Analyze US FDE and FDE-like job postings to identify repeated skills, interview patterns, location expectations, and compensation considerations.

## Learning Order

1. [examples/us-fde-job-posting-analysis.en.md](examples/us-fde-job-posting-analysis.en.md)  
   Extracts common requirements and capability patterns from representative US postings.

2. [guides/fde-interview-loop-guide.en.md](guides/fde-interview-loop-guide.en.md)  
   Explains likely interview loops and how to prepare.

3. [guides/compensation-and-location-notes.en.md](guides/compensation-and-location-notes.en.md)  
   Summarizes location, onsite, travel, seniority, and compensation considerations.

## Core Takeaway

US FDE roles repeatedly require customer-facing engineering, ambiguity tolerance, production deployment judgment, AI or data workflow experience, and the ability to communicate with both technical and business stakeholders.
