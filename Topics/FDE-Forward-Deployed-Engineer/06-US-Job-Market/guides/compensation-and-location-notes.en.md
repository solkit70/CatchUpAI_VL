# Compensation and Location Notes

## Important Caveat

Compensation and location requirements change frequently. Treat this document as a strategy guide, not as a live salary database. Before applying, re-check the specific posting and current market conditions.

## Location Patterns

US FDE roles often cluster around:

- San Francisco Bay Area
- Seattle
- New York
- Washington, DC
- major enterprise or public sector customer hubs

Some roles are hybrid or require travel. FDE work often benefits from customer proximity, especially for strategic enterprise or public sector deployments.

## Why Onsite or Travel Appears

FDE roles may require onsite work because:

- discovery is easier in the customer's environment
- security constraints limit remote access
- stakeholder alignment requires in-person workshops
- enterprise rollout needs training and enablement
- public sector work may require controlled environments

## Compensation Factors

Compensation can vary by:

- company stage and funding
- seniority
- location
- equity component
- public sector or commercial focus
- required travel
- security clearance
- technical depth

## Seniority Interpretation

| Level | Typical Expectation |
|---|---|
| Junior / Associate | Strong portfolio, basic implementation, high learning velocity |
| Mid-Level | Can independently build and deploy customer workflows |
| Senior | Owns ambiguous customer problems, architecture, stakeholder alignment |
| Staff / Lead | Converts field patterns into product strategy and repeatable playbooks |

## Candidate Strategy

Do not evaluate compensation in isolation. A lower-title role at a strong AI company may be valuable if it gives direct FDE experience, customer exposure, and portfolio evidence. A higher-paying role may be less useful if it is mostly account support without technical ownership.

## Questions to Ask Recruiters

- How much hands-on coding is expected?
- How much customer travel or onsite work is required?
- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- Is the role pre-sales, post-sales, deployment, or product feedback focused?
- What technical systems will I build or integrate?
- Are there security clearance or compliance requirements?
- How is compensation split between salary, bonus, and equity?
