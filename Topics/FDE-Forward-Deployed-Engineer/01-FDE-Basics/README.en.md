## Core Takeaway

An FDE is not just a developer who meets customers, and not just a consultant who understands technology. An FDE works inside the customer's context, translates ambiguous business workflows into working systems, helps drive production adoption, and sends field signals back into product development.

In the AI era, this role matters because model capability alone does not create business value. Enterprises need people who can connect models, data, workflow, security constraints, and user adoption in the real world.
