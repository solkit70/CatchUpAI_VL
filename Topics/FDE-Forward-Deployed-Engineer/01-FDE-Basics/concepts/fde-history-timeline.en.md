# FDE History Timeline

## Why This History Matters

FDE is not a brand-new idea invented by AI companies. The modern AI FDE role builds on earlier enterprise software patterns, especially the Palantir model of embedding technical builders near complex customer operations. What changed in the AI era is the technical substrate: LLMs, agents, RAG, evals, and AI workflow adoption now sit at the center.

## Timeline

| Period | Context | Customer Problem | FDE Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2000s | Enterprise software becomes more complex | Customers cannot simply install software and get value | Technical experts need to understand operations |
| 2010s | Palantir popularizes forward deployed work | Data platforms must fit messy government and enterprise workflows | Engineers and deployment teams work close to the customer |
| Late 2010s | Cloud and SaaS adoption expand | Enterprise integrations, security, and workflow change become blockers | Solutions engineering and architecture roles grow |
| 2020-2023 | Generative AI becomes available | Companies can demo AI but struggle to deploy it safely | AI implementation needs evals, data boundaries, and rollout design |
| 2024-2026 | AI companies hire FDE-like roles | Customers need AI inside production workflows | FDE becomes a role for AI adoption, integration, and field feedback |

## What Stayed the Same

The FDE pattern has always been about working near the real problem. The customer environment is messy, requirements are incomplete, and the product is rarely enough by itself. Someone must translate between the customer's workflow and the product's capabilities.

## What Changed in the AI Era

AI FDE work adds new technical concerns:

- model behavior is probabilistic
- evals are required to measure quality
- RAG and data integration shape system usefulness
- security and privacy boundaries are critical
- hallucination, latency, and cost must be managed
- customer adoption depends on trust and workflow fit

## From Palantir to AI FDE

Palantir-style FDE work often centered on operational data platforms and enterprise workflows. AI FDE work often centers on turning model capability into reliable customer workflow. The shape is similar: embedded problem solving, technical implementation, and product feedback. The tools and risks are different.

## The Main Lesson

FDE exists because product value does not automatically transfer from vendor to customer. The gap between product capability and customer adoption is where FDEs operate.
