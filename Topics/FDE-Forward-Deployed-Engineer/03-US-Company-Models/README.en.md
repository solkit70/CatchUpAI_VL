# M3 - US AI Company FDE Models

**Status**: Complete  
**Estimated study time**: 5h  
**Module goal**: Compare how FDE and FDE-like roles differ across US AI companies and translate those differences into candidate strategy.

## Learning Order

1. [concepts/fde-company-archetypes.en.md](concepts/fde-company-archetypes.en.md)  
   Defines major FDE archetypes across AI labs, developer tools, data/agent infrastructure, and vertical AI companies.

2. [examples/company-comparison-matrix.en.md](examples/company-comparison-matrix.en.md)  
   Compares representative companies by customer, product, technical scope, and candidate fit.

3. [guides/candidate-fit-selector.en.md](guides/candidate-fit-selector.en.md)  
   Provides a practical way for candidates to identify which FDE archetype best matches their background.

## Core Takeaway

The same title can mean different work at different companies. OpenAI-style FDE, Cursor-style FDE, Scale AI-style FDE, and vertical AI FDE roles share the customer-facing deployment pattern, but they differ in product surface, customer type, technical depth, and success metrics.
