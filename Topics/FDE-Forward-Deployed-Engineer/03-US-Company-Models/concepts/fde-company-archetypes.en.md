# FDE Company Archetypes

## Why Archetypes Matter

FDE is not one uniform job. The role depends heavily on the company's product, customer segment, go-to-market motion, and technical stack. Candidates should therefore read job postings by work pattern rather than by title alone.

## Four Main Archetypes

| Archetype | Typical Company Type | Customer | Technical Center | Candidate Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Lab FDE | Frontier model company | Enterprises, public sector, strategic customers | APIs, custom AI workflows, security, evals | Strong SWE plus customer-facing deployment |
| Developer Tool FDE | AI coding or developer platform | Engineering teams | developer workflow, IDE/tooling, debugging, evals | Developer productivity and engineering workflow background |
| Data/Agent Infrastructure FDE | Data, agent, or AI platform company | Enterprises, government, operations teams | data connectors, ETL, agents, compliance | Backend, data, platform, public sector experience |
| Vertical AI FDE | Industry-specific AI company | Finance, legal, healthcare, knowledge work | domain workflow, retrieval, review, knowledge automation | Domain plus technical implementation ability |

## OpenAI-Style FDE

OpenAI-style FDE work is often about helping strategic customers turn frontier model capability into real production workflows. The candidate needs strong engineering fundamentals, the ability to build custom software, and comfort with security, evaluation, and enterprise constraints.

Key signals:

- customer problem to custom AI system
- API and application integration
- production deployment support
- product feedback from field work

## Cursor-Style FDE

Cursor-style FDE work is closer to developer workflow transformation. The customer is often an engineering organization. The FDE must understand how software teams build, debug, review, and ship code, then help them adopt AI-native development workflows.

Key signals:

- developer workflow adoption
- debugging model behavior in coding tasks
- evals for AI-assisted engineering
- latency, cost, and usability trade-offs

## Scale AI-Style FDE

Scale AI-style FDE roles often connect data, agents, public sector or enterprise deployment, and compliance-heavy environments. The role may include data pipelines, connectors, agent workflows, operational constraints, and stakeholder coordination.

Key signals:

- data infrastructure
- agent workflows
- public sector or regulated deployment
- compliance and integration boundaries

## Vertical AI FDE

Vertical AI companies need FDEs who understand a specific domain deeply. Finance, legal, healthcare, and enterprise knowledge work often require domain-specific workflow knowledge. The FDE must translate expert workflows into AI-supported systems.

Key signals:

- domain workflow understanding
- knowledge retrieval
- review and decision support
- user trust and adoption

## How to Read a Posting

Look for verbs. FDE-like postings often use verbs such as:

- build
- deploy
- integrate
- debug
- evaluate
- unblock
- partner with customers
- generalize learnings

If the posting emphasizes only presentations, pipeline support, or account management, it may be FDE-adjacent rather than core FDE.

## Candidate Strategy

Candidates should choose a target archetype rather than applying to every FDE title. A backend engineer may fit data/agent infrastructure. A developer productivity engineer may fit developer tool FDE. A consultant with strong technical evidence may fit vertical AI or enterprise AI deployment. A non-IT domain expert may first target domain solution specialist or AI consultant roles.
