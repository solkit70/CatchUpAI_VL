# Company Comparison Matrix

## Representative FDE Models

| Company Type | Example Role Pattern | Customer | Main Work | Candidate Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frontier AI Lab | Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Software Engineer | Large enterprises, public sector, strategic partners | Build custom AI workflows, integrate APIs, support production deployment | Strong SWE, customer-facing experience, AI systems judgment |
| Developer Tool Company | FDE, Field Engineer, AI Adoption Engineer | Engineering teams | Transform software development workflow with AI tools | Developer productivity, coding workflow, debugging, enablement |
| Data/Agent Infrastructure Company | FDE, Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Applied AI Engineer | Enterprise operations, public sector, data-heavy teams | Build data connectors, agent workflows, compliance-aware deployment | Backend/data/platform skills, ambiguity tolerance |
| Vertical AI Company | FDE, Solutions Engineer, Applied AI Engineer | Finance, legal, healthcare, enterprise knowledge teams | Map domain workflow to AI assistant or review system | Domain knowledge plus technical implementation |

## Comparison Dimensions

| Dimension | AI Lab | Developer Tool | Data/Agent Infra | Vertical AI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product Surface | Model/API/platform | IDE, coding assistant, developer workflow | Data pipelines, agents, enterprise AI platform | Domain-specific workflow product |
| Primary User | Enterprise teams | Engineers | Operations or public sector teams | Domain experts |
| Technical Depth | High software engineering and AI integration | High developer workflow understanding | High data/backend/integration depth | Medium to high domain + AI workflow depth |
| Customer Interaction | Strategic and consultative | Hands-on with engineering teams | Deployment and operations focused | Embedded in domain workflow |
| Success Metric | Production AI use case | Developer adoption and productivity | Reliable workflow deployment | Knowledge work efficiency or decision quality |

## Example Fit Profiles

### Candidate A: Backend Engineer with Enterprise Integration Experience

Best fit: AI Lab FDE or Data/Agent Infrastructure FDE.  
Reason: This candidate can understand APIs, data flows, authentication, deployment constraints, and production trade-offs.

### Candidate B: Developer Productivity Engineer

Best fit: Developer Tool FDE.  
Reason: This candidate understands how engineering teams work, where AI coding tools fit, and what adoption blockers exist.

### Candidate C: Financial Services Consultant Learning AI

Best fit: Vertical AI FDE-adjacent role first, then FDE.  
Reason: Domain expertise is strong, but technical evidence must be built through prototypes, RAG systems, or review workflows.

### Candidate D: Public Sector Data Engineer

Best fit: Data/Agent Infrastructure FDE or public sector AI deployment role.  
Reason: Public sector and regulated environments value data governance, compliance, security, and operational context.

## What to Extract from Job Postings

For each posting, extract:

- role title
- customer type
- product surface
- required years of experience
- required technical stack
- customer-facing expectation
- onsite or travel expectation
- security or compliance requirement
- success metric or implied business outcome
- repeated action verbs

## Main Insight

FDE is a family of roles. The strongest candidates do not ask only "Can I be an FDE?" They ask "Which FDE archetype matches my technical background, customer experience, and domain context?"
