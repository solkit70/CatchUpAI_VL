## Key Concepts Summary

### BRD vs PRD Difference

| Item | BRD | PRD |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Focus** | Why | What & How |
| **Content** | Business goals, success metrics | Detailed feature specs, user stories |
| **Audience** | Executives, stakeholders | Dev team, designers |
| **Timing** | Project start | After BRD approval |

### Vibe Coding Concept
> "Instead of writing code line by line, developers convey their vision to an AI coding partner with clear prompts — a new development methodology"
> - Andrej Karpathy (OpenAI co-founder, February 2025)

**Core Principle**: Quality of requirements = Quality of AI-generated code
