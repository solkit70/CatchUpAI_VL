## M1 - Guide Foundation + Plain-Language Glossary

**Module**: M1 / **Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-08-10) / **Estimated time**: 1.5 hours

This folder holds the **skeleton and language standard** the whole guide follows. Every document in M2–M7 goes into a chapter slot decided here and follows the terms and review standard set here. That's why this module comes first — start writing without a standard and you'll have to redo everything later.

## 📚 Learning order

1. [01-outline.en.md](01-outline.en.md) — Full guide outline and the module mapping for each chapter. The per-tier motivation slots and Catch Up AI placement were fixed here too
2. [02-glossary.en.md](02-glossary.en.md) — Plain-language glossary of 21 terms + 16 expressions to avoid. **Every document uses these definitions as written**
3. [03-writing-checklist.en.md](03-writing-checklist.en.md) — The 8-area review list to run every time a document is finished

## What this module decided

**The guide runs 8 chapters + 4 appendices, about 30 pages.** Chapter order follows the reader's emotional order, not feature order — ease the "am I allowed?" feeling (ch 0–1), make them understand "what this is" (ch 2), get their hands moving (ch 3–4), then teach how to use it well (ch 5–6).

**Why installation is in chapter 3 and not chapter 2** matters. If you start installing without knowing what you're installing or why, a reason to give up appears the moment you get stuck.

**We don't split the document by tier.** Student, senior, and civic-group worker read the same guide, with at least one passage each where they feel "this is about me." Splitting triples maintenance and loses the benefit of learning from each other's cases.
