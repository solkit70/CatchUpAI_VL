## M7 (Capstone) - Integrated Guidebook + Facilitator Manual

**Module**: M7 / **Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-08-10) / **Estimated time**: 2 hours

Binds the outputs of M1–M6 into **two distributable books.** The two documents in this folder are this Topic's final deliverables.

## 📚 Learning order

1. [01-participant-guidebook.en.md](01-participant-guidebook.en.md) — **The document you send to the participant as-is.** Complete from welcome → concept → install → first success → using → next steps
2. [02-facilitator-manual.en.md](02-facilitator-manual.en.md) — **For the facilitator.** Before-session checks, session order and time allocation, frequently-asked questions, easily-made mistakes

## What this module decided

### Compression — 2,234 lines → 397 lines

Merging the M2–M6 bodies as-is is 2,234 lines. Left that way, no one reads to the end. **The guidebook holds only "the minimum to reach the end," and links out to each module document for detail.**

| Chapter | Original | Guidebook | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ch 2 Concept | 302 lines | ~70 lines | Essentials only. Cases compressed into tables |
| Ch 3 Install | 530 lines | ~90 lines | **Kept every step.** Trimmed explanation and troubleshooting |
| Ch 4 VibeLearn | 570 lines | ~60 lines | Kept the steps, split detail to appendix |
| Ch 5 Using it well | 426 lines | ~55 lines | Restructured around tables |
| Ch 6 Keeping at it | 406 lines | ~70 lines | Cases cut to one line each |

**The install procedure (ch 3·4) was compressed but no step was dropped.** The DoD is that the participant can reach the end with this one document. Instead, each step's background explanation and "when it doesn't work" were trimmed and sent to links.

### Why split into two books

**The participant version** holds only "what do I do." **The facilitator version** holds "how do I take this person there." Put both in one document and the participant flounders reading content that isn't about them.

### New judgments in the facilitator manual

**Raised the Chromebook check to "ask when scheduling."** Discover it on session day and the whole session is lost. With students the Chromebook ratio is high, so it especially matters.

**"Don't do it for them" as mistake #1.** If the facilitator grabs the keyboard for lack of time, the participant can't do it alone next time. The very purpose of this program collapses.

**Spelled out "the order to cut."** Running behind is inevitable. Without deciding in advance what to trim, the facilitator cuts the wrong thing on the spot. **Never cut `hello.md` verification and first success** — without those, the participant won't reopen.

**Wrote to step away at the payment step.** Hovering at the moment they enter card details is rude.

**Left the known limitations in the document.** Install-screen wording unverified, no setup video, no teacher's-case video, troubleshooting not yet based on real cases. Walk in unaware and the facilitator is caught off guard in the field.
