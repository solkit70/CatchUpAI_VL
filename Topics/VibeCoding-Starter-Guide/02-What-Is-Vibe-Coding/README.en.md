## M2 - Concepts: What Vibe Coding and AI Collaboration Are

**Module**: M2 / **Status**: ✅ Complete (2026-08-10) / **Estimated time**: 2 hours

This folder fills guidebook **chapter 2 (What is vibe coding)**. As the chapter placed before installation, its purpose is to make the participant **start knowing what and why.** Proceed without knowing what you're installing, and a reason to give up appears the moment you get stuck.

**Reading time for participants**: 10–12 minutes

## 📚 Learning order

1. [01-what-is-vibe-coding.en.md](01-what-is-vibe-coding.en.md) — The meaning, the reality that "AI doesn't do everything," the highway analogy, the teacher's case
2. [02-beyond-coding.en.md](02-beyond-coding.en.md) — It's not only coding. Three per-tier cases (student, experienced, civic group) + the difference from web chat
3. [03-ai-as-partner.en.md](03-ai-as-partner.en.md) — Tool vs. coworker comparison, make it plan first, records make AI stronger

## What this module decided

**Opened with "it used to be / now it is."** Explaining vibe coding as a technology loses the non-developer in the first paragraph. Instead we raise the problem — that "knowing what to make" and "the skill to make it" rarely land in one person — and unfold that the second has shrunk. Then the reader finds their own position.

**We didn't hide the reality.** "The AI is not a magic wand," "bolt on features without a plan and it gets hard to fix," "you need enough to steer the AI." Encouragement alone collapses at the first failure.

**The highway analogy explains why this guide exists.** AI widened only 5 of 100 miles, and **the on-ramp is still narrow.** This guide was made to walk that on-ramp together — this sentence answers "why am I reading this" for the reader.

**We split by case, not by document.** In `02-beyond-coding.en.md` we show each of the student, experienced, and civic-group examples as an actual request sentence, and at the end state **"all three are the same method."** That way they read others' cases too.

**We nailed the difference from web chat in a table.** This gets confirmed physically in M4's "first success" and is reused as evidence in M5·M6. A seed planted in chapter 2.

**We didn't write all of chapter 6 here.** At the end of `03-ai-as-partner.en.md` we raise "records make AI stronger" but hand it off with **"covered in full in chapter 6."** Explain it all in the concepts chapter and the reader tires before installation.

**Closed with "you don't have to understand it all now."** Leaving only three things to remember (give context / plan first / leave records), we hand off to installation.
