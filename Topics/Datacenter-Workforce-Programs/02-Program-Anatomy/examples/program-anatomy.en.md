## Fixed fields

Eight fields were fixed so all six programs could be measured against the same
yardstick. **No blank cells** — if a value isn't available, record "unconfirmed +
the path tried."

Operator / Funder / Target roles / Duration / Cost / **Post-completion employer** /
Region / **Application channel**

`Post-completion employer` and `Application channel` are bolded because this is
exactly where the M1 misconception splits programs apart.

---

## ① Meta — America's Workforce Academy (AWA)

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Led by Meta, run jointly with **ABC (Associated Builders and Contractors) + CBRE** | Secondary |
| Funder | Meta — **$115M** in year one | Official |
| Target roles | Two tracks: **construction trades** (electrical, mechanical, plumbing, welding) / **fiber technician** | Official |
| Duration | **4 weeks** (official) vs. 5 weeks (secondary) — ⚠️ discrepancy | Official/Secondary |
| Cost | **Fully free** — "tuition, airfare, lodging, and a daily stipend during training" | Official |
| **Post-completion employer** | **Not Meta.** "a job at a Meta partner waiting on the other side" — a conditional hire commitment from a partner company is secured **before** training even begins | Official/Secondary |
| Region | **Open to all 50 states**, with airfare and lodging provided. 2026 pilot sites: Baton Rouge LA, Indianapolis IN, Houston TX, Columbus OH (secondary) | Official/Secondary |
| **Application channel** | Track-specific application portal on Meta's official page (construction and fiber are separate) | Official |

**Eligibility**: 18+, authorized to work in the US, able to attend full-time
training. **No experience required.**
Target: "veterans, recent graduates, career changers and other new entrants to the trades"
**Credentials earned**: NCCER credential + America's Workforce Certificate (secondary)

> 💡 **This is the only program with essentially no location restriction.** Open to
> all states, with airfare and lodging covered, so living in Washington State is not
> a disadvantage. An important item for the M7 fit assessment.

## ② Microsoft — Datacenter Academy

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | **Each community college** (Microsoft provides curriculum, equipment, scholarships, internships) | Official |
| Funder | Microsoft (scholarship — tuition, books, exam fees) | Official |
| Target roles | Data center technician, network/systems administration | Official |
| Duration | 1-year certificate or 2-year associate degree (varies by institution) | Official (BBCC) |
| Cost | College tuition — **Microsoft scholarship covers tuition, books, registration, and exam fees** | Official |
| **Post-completion employer** | Not stated. Only says it **provides Microsoft data center internship opportunities** | Official |
| Region | Multiple community colleges — **Big Bend CC (Moses Lake, WA)** · Glendale CC/Estrella Mountain CC (Phoenix AZ) · Des Moines Area CC (IA) · Northwest Vista (TX) · West Georgia Tech (GA) · Laramie County CC (WY) | Official |
| **Application channel** | **Not Microsoft. Apply directly to the college's admissions office** — "visit the college in your selected area and submit your application directly to them" | Official |

> 💡 **This is where the Washington State option lives.** Big Bend Community College
> (Moses Lake) has run this since 2018, and Microsoft provides scholarships for
> students in the **Systems Administration – Datacenter Specialization** certificate
> program. It's tied to the Quincy data centers. → Target for the M5 deep dive.

## ③ Microsoft — NABTU Partnership

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | **NABTU** (North America's Building Trades Unions) — Microsoft is the partner | Official |
| Funder | Microsoft | Official |
| Target roles | Construction trades broadly (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, etc.) | Secondary |
| Duration | Unconfirmed — apprenticeship length varies by local and trade | — |
| Cost | The AI-literacy course is **free** ("no-cost") | Official |
| **Post-completion employer** | Employment with a union construction company via apprenticeship (not Microsoft) | Inferred |
| Region | TradesFutures apprenticeship-readiness network — **34 states** | Official |
| **Application channel** | **TradesFutures apprenticeship-readiness program / local union** | Official |

⚠️ **The character here differs from the baseline table.** The 8/19 baseline table
described this entry as "electrical/plumbing/HVAC construction trades / union
apprenticeship and local training expansion," but **the actual substance of the
2026-04-21 expansion is AI-literacy education** ("no-cost AI literacy courses and
industry-recognized credentials"). It is not trades training itself, but
**teaching AI to people who are already in the trades.**

So this entry is less a "data center employment program" and more **supplementary
training layered onto an existing apprenticeship network.** The actual path to data
center employment is the **TradesFutures apprenticeship-readiness program.**

## ⑤ Amazon/AWS — Technical Workforce Development (researched 2026-08-28)

> **"Technical Workforce Development" was not Amazon's actual official program name.**
> The real umbrella brand is **`AWS Grow Our Own Talent`**, under which sit 4 paths.
> M1's read that this entry "straddles two cells" was correct — because it was
> several things, not one.

### The umbrella structure — AWS Grow Our Own Talent

| # | Path | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **⑤-a** | **Work-Based Learning Program (WBLP)** | **12-month paid training → full-time AWS data center employment** |
| ⑤-b | College internships | For students currently enrolled |
| ⑤-c | Amazon Dedicated Cloud | For cleared personnel |
| ⑤-d | Current employee opportunities | Internal transfer |

**Only ⑤-a applies to me.** The rest target current students, already-cleared
professionals, or existing employees.

### ⑤-a WBLP — 8-field breakdown

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | **Amazon/AWS directly** | Official |
| Funder | Not stated (presumed self-funded) | — |
| Target roles | **Data center operations technician · install technician · decommissioning technician · logistics specialist** (4 roles) | Official |
| Duration | **12-month paid training** | Official |
| Cost | **Free + paid** | Official |
| **Post-completion employer** | **Direct AWS employment** — *"promoted into their chosen roles at an AWS data center"* | Official |
| Region | The umbrella page names **Haymarket VA · Kent WA · Aurora CO** | Official |
| **Application channel** | **Amazon careers site** (View open roles) | Official |

### Eligibility

The umbrella page describes the target as *"students, high school or college
graduates, current employees, or cleared professionals."* Graduate interviews
include cases of **applying right out of high school with no experience.**

**No degree or experience requirement is stated.** This matches M1's read that
"Data Center Technician has the lowest barrier to entry."

### 🔑 What this research changes

**⑤-a WBLP is the closest match to my own situation among all 6 programs.**

| Basis | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Region** | **Kent, WA** — Washington State. The only confirmed WA-based path among the six |
| **Employer** | Direct AWS employment. Unlike ④ Google (via an IBEW local) or ① Meta (via a partner company), there's no intermediate step |
| **Barrier** | No degree or experience requirement |
| **Pay** | Paid starting from the training period |
| **Role match** | 4 data-center operations technician roles — not construction-phase trades |

→ **A priority target for M5 (WA deep dive) and M7 (fit assessment).** Whether the
Kent site actually has open postings gets confirmed in M5.

## ⑥ Amazon/AWS — Technical Apprenticeship

| Field | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Amazon/AWS directly | Official |
| Funder | Amazon | Official |
| Target roles | Associate Cloud Consultant · Cloud Support Associate · Solutions Architect · Software Development Engineer · Support Engineer · Technical Account Manager · Technical Sales (**all 7 are cloud roles**) | Official (re-confirmed 2026-08-28) |
| Duration | 10–18 weeks of classroom-style training + **up to 12 months of paid on-the-job training** (up to 18 months total) | Official |
| Cost | **Free + paid** — "paid training course coupled with on-the-job training" | Official |
| **Post-completion employer** | **Direct Amazon/AWS employment** (apprentices are hired directly) | Official |
| Region | No list on the official page. An article mentions Washington D.C. and North Carolina, and there is a **remote option for military spouses** | Secondary |
| **Application channel** | Amazon careers site | Official |

**A US Department of Labor (DOL)-certified program. No experience required.**

### ⚠️ Eligibility — re-confirmed 2026-08-28 (conflict unresolved)

Checked the official careers page directly again. **The conflict remains.**

| Source | Type | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| `amazon.jobs` official program page | **Primary** | *"helps members of the military community—veterans and their spouses"* — **only military-focused wording, no mention of expansion** |
| `aboutamazon` article | Secondary | *"recently expanded beyond military veterans and their spouses"* — but **does not state how far the expansion goes** |

**Verdict: by the official page, this is still targeted at the military
community.** Only a secondary source claims expansion, and even that source
doesn't specify the new scope. As long as the primary source isn't updated,
**there is no basis to consider this open to me (a non-veteran).**

### ⚠️ Correction on target roles — Data Center Technician is not on the list

Earlier research had listed **Data Center Technician** as a target role, but
**it is not among the current 7 tracks on the official page.**

| Current 7 tracks (official page) | Length |
|---|---|
| Associate Cloud Consultant | 12 weeks |
| Cloud Support Associate | 14 weeks |
| Solutions Architect | 14 weeks |
| Software Development Engineer | 18 weeks |
| Support Engineer | 12 weeks |
| Technical Account Manager | 12 weeks |
| Technical Sales | 10 weeks |

**All of them are cloud roles. Not one is data-center hands-on work.** The
`aboutamazon` article mentions a Data Center Technician apprentice case (applied
in 2021), but the official page gives no basis that this is still an open track today.

### → Conclusion on ⑥

**For my situation, ⑥ is effectively not applicable.** Two things overlap here.

1. **Eligibility** — officially targeted at the military community
2. **Role match** — current tracks are all cloud roles, which is off-target from
   this topic's goal (data center work)

**Kept as a shortlist-exclusion candidate for M7.** Not removed entirely, though —
since the expansion wording could eventually be reflected and a data-center track
could reopen, the actual postings get one more check in M5.
