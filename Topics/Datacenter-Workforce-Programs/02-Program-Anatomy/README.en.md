## What this module found

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No official deadline exists anywhere** | Not a research gap — a structural fact; most programs are run through local institutions |
| 2 | **Intake structure splits into three types** — national self-run / college academic calendar / union apprenticeship schedule | Only ①⑥ have anything resembling a "deadline" concept |
| 3 | **M1 hypothesis confirmed — 5 of 6 have separate operator/funder/employer** | Applying to the company whose name is on the program is often wrong |
| 4 | **④ Google.org's application channel is the IBEW local** | There's nowhere to apply to Google — it only funded the program |
| 5 | **③ MS NABTU's 2026 expansion was AI-literacy training** | Different in character from the "construction trades training" in the baseline table. The actual apprenticeship channel is TradesFutures |
| 6 | **① Meta AWA has essentially no location restriction** — all 50 states, with airfare/lodging/stipend | Living in WA is not a disadvantage. Important for M7 |
| 7 | **① Meta AWA locks in a job before training starts** — conditional partner-company hire commitment | Unique among the 6 — though the employer is not Meta itself |
| 8 | **⑥ Amazon is the only one where name = operator = employer** | This is the shape to target if the goal is direct hire by Big Tech |
| 9 | **⑥ Eligibility claims conflict** — "veterans and spouses" wording vs. an "expanded eligibility" news article | Can't determine personal eligibility until confirmed |
| 10 | **② A WA option exists** — Big Bend CC (Moses Lake), tied to the Quincy data centers, running since 2018 | Target for M5's deep dive |
