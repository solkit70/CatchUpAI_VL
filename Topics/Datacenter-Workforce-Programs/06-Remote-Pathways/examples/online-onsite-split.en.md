# Online / Onsite Split — BBCC Data-Center Program

**Research date**: 2026-08-30 (M6 Exercise 1)  
**Source**: BBCC 2026–2027 official catalog

## Official program name

The catalog—not the public program page—names the qualification **Data Center IT Specialist Certificate of Accomplishment (30 credits)**.

| Course | Credits | Lecture | Lab | Remote likelihood |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| CS 103 Hardware & Operating Systems | 6 | 22h | 66h | Low |
| CS 116 / 117 Network Security I / II | 6 | — | 44h | High |
| CS 120 A+ Prep & Certification | 1–2 | — | 22h | Low–medium |
| CS 171 Cisco Intro to Networks | 6 | 22h | 66h | High |
| CS 205 Windows Server Administration | 5 | 22h | 22h | High |
| CS 206 Linux Server Administration | 5 | 22h | 22h | High |
| **Total** | **30** | **88h** | **242h** | **73% lab** |

## The number must not be misread

**73% lab does not mean 73% onsite attendance.** In a college catalog, lab denotes a practical class format. Networking and server work commonly use Packet Tracer, NETLAB, and virtual machines. CS 103 is the critical question because disassembly/assembly of real hardware may be required.

BBCC supports online (`OL`), hybrid (`H`), and arranged online classes. The school therefore supports remote students; the unanswered question is whether these seven particular courses are offered that way.

## Known onsite elements

Work-based learning gives hands-on experience with partner facilities but its required status is unknown. The 30-credit program contains no FAD 150/CPR barrier; that requirement belongs to the larger Systems Administration certificate.

The next action is to ask BBCC about course delivery, CS 103 hardware, and work-based-learning location.
