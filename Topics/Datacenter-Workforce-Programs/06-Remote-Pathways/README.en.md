# M6 — Remote and Online Pathways

**Period**: 2026-08-30 (one day)  
**Prerequisites**: [M5](../05-WA-Deep-Dive/README.en.md) · [M4](../04-US-Regional-Map/README.en.md)

## Why this matters now

AWS WBLP had no U.S. openings, PSEJATC intake was suspended, and BBCC was the open route—but with a relocation and unpaid-training burden. M6 asks the precise question: **how much of the curriculum can be completed remotely?**

## Confirmed

BBCC’s official catalog identifies the program as **Data Center IT Specialist Certificate of Accomplishment**: 30 credits and seven CS courses. It totals 88 lecture hours and 242 lab hours (73% lab), with CS 103 Hardware & Operating Systems the largest course at 66 lab hours.

Lab hours describe instructional format, not location. Cisco, server-administration, and network-security work can often use virtual labs and VMs; hardware work and A+ preparation are less likely to be fully remote. BBCC already offers virtual, in-person, and hybrid classes using Canvas and Zoom, but it does not publish delivery modes for these seven courses.

## What cannot simply be replaced online

1. Work-based learning with a data-center partner may require onsite work; whether it is required is unconfirmed.
2. FAD 150 first-aid/CPR applies to the broader Systems Administration certificate, **not** the 30-credit Data Center IT Specialist certificate.
3. CS 103’s 66 hardware lab hours may require physical components.

## Remote prerequisites to start now

| Option | Cost | Delivery |
|---|---:|---|
| CompTIA A+ | $548 for two exams | At-home online proctoring available |
| CompTIA Network+ | $399 list price | At-home online proctoring available |
| Cisco Packet Tracer / NetAcad intro | Free | Online virtual labs |
| Microsoft Learn / Linux VM practice | Free | Online |

Earning a credential and receiving college credit for it are different things. Credit for prior learning has not been confirmed.

## Required BBCC questions

Ask whether each course is offered online/hybrid, whether CS 103 requires physical hardware, whether work-based learning is mandatory and can occur near the student’s home, and whether prior A+/Network+ credentials earn credit.
