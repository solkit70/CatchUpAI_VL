# Meta AWA Curriculum — What Is Confirmed

Meta America’s Workforce Academy provides four weeks of paid, intensive skilled-trade training. The program is free and covers transportation and lodging; acceptance includes a conditional job offer with a Meta partner. Public materials identify two paths: **Fiber Technician** and **Construction Ready**.

The Fiber path was preferred because diagnosis and troubleshooting connect to prior IT/infrastructure work, and its portal names Indianapolis, IN and Columbus, OH as training locations. The broader four-state announcement also includes Louisiana and Texas, likely for construction-related delivery, but this was not confirmed.

Public materials do not confirm cohort start date, city assignment method, daily stipend amount, lodging type, physical requirements, background/drug-screening requirements, or whether fusion splicing and OTDR testing are taught. Ask these questions if invited to interview or accepted.
