# Meta AWA Fiber Track — English Résumé Strategy and Draft

**Use**: Supporting rationale for the submit-ready document `Changsoo Park - Resume - AWA Fiber Technician.docx`.

## Strategy

AWA is not evaluating a conventional direct-hire résumé. It asks whether an applicant will reliably complete four weeks of training, learn, travel, and stay in the work. A long IT career can invite the question, “Why this program?” The résumé therefore foregrounds demonstrated mobility, methodical troubleshooting, continued learning, and procedure discipline.

The strongest evidence is a work history spanning South Korea and six U.S. states over fifteen years—repeated relocation for long client assignments—not a generic promise to travel. The 2024 **Cloud Infrastructure Engineer** title supplies a credible adjacent bridge to infrastructure work.

## Required résumé facts to verify before submission

- Phone number and work authorization
- Gangwon National University major and graduation year
- Washington driver-license status
- Exact titles, dates, and locations for all roles

The final résumé should retain factual employment history while making clear: available for full-time training in Indianapolis or Columbus; Korean native speaker; professional working English; hands-on diagnostics, test automation, cloud-infrastructure support, and documented repeatable procedures.

Do not add inferred facts, credentials, or employment claims.
