# Direct-Entry Assessment — Supporting Material Outside the Topic Scope

This document is retained as an appendix, not as a recommended workstream. The topic is about training programs supported by big tech and connected to jobs—not general job-market searching.

Two findings remain useful: Washington data centers cluster around Quincy, East Wenatchee, and Moses Lake, explaining BBCC’s location; and general AWS Data Center Technician listings require two years of relevant hardware/networking experience **or related IT experience**. That changes WBLP’s value: it is not necessarily the only way over an entry threshold, but it remains unusually attractive for its paid training, location, and direct-employment structure.

Any direct job search should be treated as a separately authorized effort.
