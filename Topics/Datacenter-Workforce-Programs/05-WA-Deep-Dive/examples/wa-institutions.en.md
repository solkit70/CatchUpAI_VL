## Big Bend Community College

Located in Moses Lake, BBCC offers a data-center technician certificate that can be completed in under a year, with related Network Support Specialist and Systems Administration certificates. Its stated goal is entry-level IT networking and data-center technician work. Fall is the standard new-cohort start, with winter/spring options possible. Microsoft scholarships may cover tuition, books, and exam fees, but the actual tuition was not published.

The verified contact route is `computerscience@bigbend.edu` and `(509) 793-2222`; a previously recorded individual email bounced and should not be used without reconfirmation.

## Puget Sound Electrical JATC

PSEJATC in Renton offers three electrical pathways under WSATC registration. The Inside Wire program entails 8,000 field hours plus 96 class hours. New applications have been suspended since 2026-05-01; reopening requires public notice at least 30 days in advance. Application is fully online, costs a nonrefundable $75, requires a valid Washington driver license, and requires eligibility documents within 60 days of opening.

## AWS data center, Kent

The Work-Based Learning Program is a paid twelve-month path toward regular employment, but there were no Washington openings. On 2026-08-30 the Ohio opening observed two days earlier had also disappeared, leaving three international listings only.

Actual WBLP requirements confirmed: high-school diploma/equivalent, age 18+, shift work, and ability to lift up to 18 kg. General DCT positions instead require two years of relevant troubleshooting and Layer 1/2 networking experience.
