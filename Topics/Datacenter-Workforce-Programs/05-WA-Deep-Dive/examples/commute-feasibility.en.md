## Reference point

Residence: **near Bonney Lake / Orting, Pierce County, WA**. Distances and travel times below are geographic estimates, not route-service measurements.

## Classification

| Institution | Location | Estimated distance | Classification | Rationale |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| AWS data center | Kent, WA | 20–25 mi | ✅ Commute feasible | Same Puget Sound area; typically 30–40 minutes |
| Puget Sound Electrical JATC | Renton, WA | 20–25 mi | ✅ Commute feasible | Same region; near Kent |
| Big Bend Community College | Moses Lake, WA | 180 mi | 🔴 Relocation or weekday stay | Across the Cascades; about three hours each way |

## What this reveals

Data centers cluster in central Washington, where power is inexpensive, while the residence is in Puget Sound. Yet the research showed that **training is far away and employment can be close by**: BBCC is the distant education route, while AWS Kent and PSEJATC Renton are local work routes.

## If BBCC requires a stay

Housing cost in Moses Lake, duplicated living costs, weekend travel, and the opportunity cost of a year without income must be included. Even zero tuition does not mean zero cost.

| | AWS WBLP | PSEJATC | BBCC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residence | Stay home | Stay home | Relocate / temporary stay |
| Training income | Paid | Paid | None |
| Duration | 12 months | 8,000 hours / 4–5 years | One year |
| Available now | No WA posting | Intake suspended | Fall cohort open |
