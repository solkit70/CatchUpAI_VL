## Summary

This is where the inquiry becomes personal: not “what exists?” but **“what can I apply to now?”** On 2026-08-28, the two routes with the best living conditions were closed, while the open one required relocation and provided no training income.

| Path | Status | Living conditions |
|---|---|---|
| AWS WBLP, Kent | ❌ No WA posting | Commutable; paid; 12 months |
| PSEJATC electrical apprenticeship, Renton | ❌ Intake suspended from 2026-05-01 | Commutable; paid; 4–5 years |
| BBCC data-center program, Moses Lake | ✅ Fall cohort open | Relocation needed; unpaid; one year |

## Key findings

1. **WBLP is not continuous hiring.** A live Amazon Jobs API search found four worldwide WBLP posts, only one in the United States (Ohio), and none in Washington.
2. Actual WBLP postings confirmed the baseline requirements: high-school diploma or equivalent, age 18+, shift rotations for 24/7 operations, and ability to lift up to 18 kg/40 lb. No prior experience requirement was stated.
3. General Data Center Technician listings require two or more years of hardware and Layer 1/2 networking experience. WBLP is therefore a scarce bridge across that threshold.
4. Union apprenticeship intake opens and closes. PSEJATC suspended new applications on 2026-05-01 and must announce reopening at least 30 days ahead.
5. The distance problem runs backward from expectation: BBCC training is about 180 miles away, while AWS Kent and PSEJATC Renton are about 20–25 miles away.
