## Why separate this case

**What fails for me may be the best option for someone else.** I live in western Washington, need income now, and have about 20 years of IT experience. Those conditions changed every decision; use the [public guide](../public-guide.en.md) for general guidance.

## Result

| # | Program | Outcome | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meta AWA / LevelUp | ✅ Submitted | Free, paid, four weeks, conditional job offer; résumé was the only document |
| 2 | BBCC | Lowered to fourth | Not an immediate-income path |
| 3 | NABTU | Closed by inference | SW WA JATC’s real conditions answered the relevant question |
| 4 | SW WA JATC | Information gathering | Five years; 40% starting wage |
| 5 | AWS WBLP | Automated monitoring | WA remained at zero posts across three checks |
| 6 | Amazon Technical Apprenticeship | ❌ Ineligible | Veteran/military-spouse only |

The decisive statement was: “My purpose is income coming in now.” A one-year, self-funded program without guaranteed employment changed from an advantage to a disqualifier.

## Lessons

1. The bottleneck was geography, not credentials: no Washington AWS WBLP posting and no WA option in Meta’s preferred-location dropdown.
2. Research one neighboring item deeply and it may settle another; SW WA JATC conditions made separate NABTU investigation unnecessary.
3. Job titles can mislead: Inside Wireman is building electrician work, not software work.
4. Do not let AI infer dates, residency, or eligibility; verify them directly.
5. Cross-check program contact details against a live staff directory: both BBCC addresses used for the inquiry bounced.
