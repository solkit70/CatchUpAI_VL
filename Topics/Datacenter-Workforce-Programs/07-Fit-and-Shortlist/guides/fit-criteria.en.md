# Decision Criteria and Elimination Lines

**Confirmed**: 2026-08-30 (with a 2026-08-31 exception)

1. **Entry requirements**: eliminate paths requiring a degree, relevant experience, military service, or clearance that the applicant does not have.
2. **Intake timing**: a temporarily closed program is **wait**, not failure; a discontinued program fails.
3. **Commute/stay**: fail if three or more local overnight stays per week are required; allow one or two commute days weekly and short intensive stays. For commute programs, use a 200-mile one-way ceiling from Bonney Lake.
4. **Income during training**: fail if unpaid time exceeds 12 months. Paid apprenticeships are not limited.
5. **Duration**: fail if unpaid time exceeds two years; paid training has no duration limit.
6. **Physical demand**: no elimination line; shifts, 18 kg lifting, and construction work are acceptable.
7. **Compatibility**: no elimination line; full-time participation is acceptable.
8. **Cost**: fail above $10,000 out of pocket, less confirmed scholarships/support. This is a conservative assumption still requiring user confirmation.

### Intensive-stay exception

A one-time, full-time out-of-state stay is allowed only when all four conditions hold: no more than four consecutive weeks and not recurring; travel/lodging paid by the program; income during training; and guaranteed or conditional post-completion employment. This was created for Meta AWA and must not be applied broadly.
