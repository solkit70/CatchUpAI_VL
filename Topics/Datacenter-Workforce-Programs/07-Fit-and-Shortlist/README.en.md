# M7 — Personal Fit Assessment and Final Shortlist

**Period**: 2026-08-30 (one day)  
**Prerequisite**: M1–M6

## What M7 did

It applied eight criteria, set **before looking at candidates**, to all twelve pathways. The result: three pass, three wait, and six fail.

The key correction was that “no current relocation plan” did not mean “cannot travel 180 miles.” The user could commute once or twice weekly, absorb 6–12 months without income, work full time, and accept construction physical demands.

| Result | Count |
|---|---:|
| ✅ Pass | 3 (all BBCC) |
| ⏸ Wait | 3 |
| ❌ Fail | 6 |

Entry requirements eliminated five paths; physical demands and schedule compatibility eliminated none. The real constraints are **location and intake timing**.
