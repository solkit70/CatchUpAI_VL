# Shortlist — Final Three and Track Conclusion

**Confirmed**: 2026-08-30

## 1. AWS WBLP (Kent, WA)

No degree or experience requirement, a 25-mile commute, paid twelve-month training, and direct AWS employment make this the best structural fit. It is unavailable only because there were no U.S. postings. Set an alert and apply immediately when it opens.

## 2. BBCC Data Center IT Specialist Certificate

This was the only then-open route. The 30-credit, seven-CS-course option is lighter than BBCC Systems Administration because it has no general education or FAD 150 CPR requirement. It remains conditional on tuition and whether its remote/onsite structure requires three or more weekly stays in Moses Lake.

## 3. PSEJATC Electrical Apprenticeship

It is paid, local, and physically acceptable, but new applications have been suspended since 2026-05-01. Register for its reopening notice.

## Track conclusion

Use operations technical work as the primary track and skilled trades as an active backup. The two technical options are structurally close to home or have a clear entry bridge; skilled trades remain strong because they pay during training and may rise in priority when PSEJATC reopens.

## Reconsider when

- A U.S. WBLP post appears
- PSEJATC reopens
- BBCC requires three or more in-person days weekly or costs more than $10,000 out of pocket
- Meta expands AWA to the West
