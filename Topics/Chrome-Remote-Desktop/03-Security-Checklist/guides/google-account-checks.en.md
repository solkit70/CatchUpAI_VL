## Before You Start

**Why check this first**: Your Google Account is the first key to accessing your computer through Chrome Remote Desktop. Do not write or capture your email address, phone number, recovery email, or device location here; record **status only**.

## 1. Two-Step Verification

1. Open [Google Account Security](https://myaccount.google.com/security).
2. Under **How you sign in to Google**, open **2-Step Verification**.
3. Confirm that it is on.
4. Confirm that you have a usable backup method. Do not store the backup codes in this topic.

Google says a second step can help protect your account even if your password is exposed. Source: [Protect your personal information with 2-Step Verification](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/10956730?hl=en)

## 2. Devices Signed In to Your Account

1. Open [Your devices](https://google.com/devices).
2. Review devices and sessions currently signed in or used in recent weeks.
3. Consider device type, browser, recent activity time, and approximate location together; do not rely on the name alone.
4. Before removing an unfamiliar-looking entry, check whether it could be another browser or app session of yours.

A device can have multiple sessions for different browsers or apps. If you confirm a device is unknown, sign it out and follow the account security steps. Source: [See devices with account access](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3067630?hl=en)

## 3. Recent Security Activity

1. Open **Recent security activity** on the Google Account Security page.
2. Check whether each sign-in or security-setting change was yours.
3. If you see activity you do not recognize, select **No, it wasn’t me** and follow the instructions to secure your account.

Google recommends reviewing recent security events together with signed-in devices. Source: [Help secure a hacked or compromised Google Account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/6294825?hl=en)

## 4. Recovery Information

1. Confirm that your recovery phone number and email are still usable.
2. Do not put the actual address or number shown on screen into a WorkLog or screenshot.
3. If something needs changing, stop this checklist and update it yourself after completing the required verification.

Recovery information can help you regain access if you lose your phone or cannot sign in. Source: [Fix common issues with 2-Step Verification](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185834?hl=en)
