## Using the Checklist

The user reported completing this checklist. Conditions confirmed in this practice included being at home with iPhone Wi-Fi off, reading and approving a document, entering a remote message, Disconnecting and reconnecting, and verifying the result of a 45-second background task. The [test log](../examples/loop-session-log.en.md) records the details.

- [ ] Plug in the laptop. If its battery runs out, you cannot reconnect.
- [ ] Check the sleep setting while plugged in. If the computer sleeps, remote access may be interrupted.
- [ ] Leave the review document open in Obsidian and the AI conversation open on the laptop.
- [ ] Decide in one sentence what follow-up task to give the AI. This time: write and record M5 practice documents.
- [ ] Check whether the task includes anything needing a separate decision, such as publishing, sending, or deleting.
- [ ] Record the iPhone connection and location. Distinguish mobile data from Wi-Fi and whether you are away from home.
- [ ] Prepare a short instruction: “Content approved; continue to the next practice” or “Please revise only this sentence.”
- [ ] When finishing, use Windows Lock → confirm the lock screen → Disconnect.

Earlier verification of power and exit procedures is in the [M4 iPhone test](../../vl_worklog/20260923_M4_Chrome-Remote-Desktop.md#activity-4--first-iphone-mobile-data-test). Whether the checklist itself was physically used during this session was checked separately.
