## Current Assumption

On 2026-09-27, the user decided not to install another remote-access app. RustDesk therefore remains a comparison candidate; there is no second tool ready for immediate use when CRD fails. Do not rush to change router settings or accept remote-support offers from strangers to resolve an outage.

## Troubleshooting Sequence

1. Check that Wi-Fi or mobile data on the iPhone works. If only one connection is having trouble, open the CRD access page again using the other connection.
2. Check that the home laptop is powered on, connected to power and the internet, and not asleep. If you are away and cannot check it directly, ask someone at home only to check its status.
3. Confirm that CRD on the iPhone uses the **same Google Account** registered on the laptop. If the computer appears but will not connect, record the on-screen error; do not keep guessing PINs.
4. When you can access the home laptop, check its host service and network using the [M2 troubleshooting table](../../02-Install-and-First-Connect/troubleshooting/cannot-connect.en.md).
5. If you cannot view the home laptop right now, continue only work available directly from your phone through documents or services you can open. Pause tasks that require remote Windows. Do not give secret values or personal files to a temporary remote-support service.

## If You Reconsider Installing an Alternative
