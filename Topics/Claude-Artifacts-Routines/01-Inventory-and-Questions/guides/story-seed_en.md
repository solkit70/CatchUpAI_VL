## Three sentences

1. **I encountered them while working.** I learned about these features while researching Datacenter and Caregiver jobs; I had not set out to study the features. See [[Journal/2026-09-01#Learnings|the September 1 Journal entry]].
2. **I did not need to know the feature names.** I had assumed I needed enough prior knowledge to direct a tool well. Instead, AI found and applied features I had never heard of; a clear goal mattered more than prior feature knowledge. See the same [[Journal/2026-09-01#Learnings|Journal reflection]].
3. **I built something during a meeting and used it in that meeting.** The difference was not simply speed; the idea was built while its context was still present. See [[Journal/2026-09-03|the September 3 Journal entry]].

## What the video should avoid

Avoid listing features. Pages A5 and A6 already explain them. The video should follow the three-part story—encounter, not needing prior knowledge, and building on the spot—with the features appearing in context. This was the starting point for M6 exercise 1.
