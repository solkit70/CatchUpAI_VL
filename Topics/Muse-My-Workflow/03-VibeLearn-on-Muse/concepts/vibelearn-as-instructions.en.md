# What It Means to Read and Follow Instructions

[한국어](vibelearn-as-instructions.md)

## Starting from a Public Repository

Muse could start a Topic by reading the instructions and templates in the public repository. Whether it followed the instructions is checked by whether it asked one question at a time, whether it stopped at the user confirmation points, and whether the original instructions remained in the prompts it generated. ## What Is Needed to Pick Up at Home

The next agent needs the Topic info, prompts that hold the instructions in full, and the step to resume from. This time, after downloading the Topic info and the two prompts, the next action could be decided by reading the prompt's duration review instruction and the current input.
