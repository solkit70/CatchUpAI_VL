# Fit Judgment Criteria — Which Work to Delegate to Muse
[한국어](fit-criteria.md)

> These are the criteria for deciding, task by task, whether to delegate it to Muse. "Muse can do it" and "it is better to delegate it to Muse" are different. Work that goes better inside the vault with Claude Code · Codex gives little reason to delegate to Muse.

## 3 Fit Levels

| Level | Meaning | What to do at home |
|---|---|---|
| **Delegate** | Use Muse's result almost as is | Move it into the vault and only mark the source |
| **Draft only** | Muse makes a draft or note, and a person finishes it | After moving it: review, fix, and match vault rules |
| **Don't delegate** | Must not be uploaded, or the vault / local tools are essential | Do it at home from the start |

## 4 Judgment Criteria

| # | Criterion | Question | Signal toward "Don't delegate" |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① | **Data grade** | Is it public / personal / internal? | Internal (`visibility: internal` · 🔒) · other people's contacts · client information |
| ② | **How much vault context is needed** | Does it require reading many vault documents? | Work that has to scan the whole vault (Daily Roundup · Weekly Progress) |
| ③ | **Is there a reason Muse is better?** | When away from home · by voice · through connectors (mail · schedule) · keeps going after the app is closed (background) — does even one of these apply? | Work that goes better at home with the vault and local tools |
| ④ | **Effort to move it** | Does it take a lot of work to put the result into the vault? | Work whose format rules are strict, so rewriting it at home is faster |

**Data grade**: public (broadcasts · public repos · public events) · personal (my thoughts · my to-dos · my schedule) · internal (an organization's internal documents · clients · other people's contacts). **Internal is "Don't delegate" regardless of the other criteria.** For work mixed with internal material, check separately whether the public part alone can be split off and delegated — [../guides/do-not-upload.en.md](../guides/do-not-upload.en.md).

**v2.0 change (2026-10-25)**: ③ was first "Does it happen away from home?" The Topic's scope widened from "when I don't have a laptop" to "all my work," so I changed it: even if not away from home, a task can be delegated if there is a reason Muse does it better — voice · connectors · background. ② was rated on the assumption that Muse cannot see the vault. When a vault copy is connected in M4, the tasks with ② rated "a lot" will be judged again.

## Instruction Style — Voice and Text (2026-10-18 Changsoo Park)

In the car, I instruct Muse by **voice**; at event venues and cafes, by **text**. Voice input itself works (verified hands-on, 2026-10-22). What remains to check is whether what I say is taken down word for word and kept as a file (preserving the original wording), and whether Muse over-inflates a short text note. Both are actually tested in M2 · M5.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **A share link is not a move.** It has entered the 2nd Brain only when it remains as a file in the vault (M2).
- **"Draft only" is not a failure.** Just capturing a thought without losing it while away from home has value. It is the level this Topic will use most.
- **Connecting is the same as having already uploaded.** If you connect mail · calendar · storage, Muse reaches inside them regardless of the "don't upload" rule — see the connection rule in [do-not-upload.en.md](../guides/do-not-upload.en.md).
