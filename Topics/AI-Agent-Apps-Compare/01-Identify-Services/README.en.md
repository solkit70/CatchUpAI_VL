## What This Module Established

The four comparison targets are Grok Bot (xAI), Muse (Meta), Dots (OpenAI), and Instinct (Spear Street Technology). Their actual tasks, official access routes, payment requirements, and invitation conditions differ, so we distinguish official materials from secondary sources rather than assuming they are the same kind of service. Muse is the service currently being prepared for direct use; Dots is limited to research and comparison because it is unavailable on ChatGPT Plus, and Instinct remains research-only until waitlist approval.
