## Card 1 — Grok Bot (xAI)

- **Official documentation**: [docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)
- **What it is**: A persistent agent with a name and role. It works across apps using the browser, files, and terminal on a dedicated cloud computer, continuing even after the laptop is closed. Multiple Bots can collaborate.
- **Approval**: Draft approval before sending; “comes back and asks when approval is needed” (official documentation).
- **Access**: “Included in paid Cursor individual plans and Cursor Teams,” or an individual SuperGrok subscription. No free plan.
- **What it is not**: The ordinary **Grok** chatbot (grok.com, X app). Grok can be used free, but Grok Bot is a separate product (official FAQ).
- ⚠️ **Changes/discrepancies**: Sep 4 research and some summary sites said “SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra only,” but the Oct 4 official documentation says “all paid Cursor individual plans” — follow the official documentation.

## Card 2 — Muse (Meta)

- **Official announcement**: [about.fb.com — Introducing Muse](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)
- **What it is**: A personal agent described as “not just answering questions, but actually doing the work.” Sending email, booking travel, filling forms, and managing long-term goals. It continues working after the app closes and notifies you when needed.
- **Approval**: Acts after user approval. Payments use Stripe Link.
- **Privacy**: Meta's announcement says conversation and VM data are not shared with advertising systems, and users can opt out of model training on interactions. Account-specific settings require separate verification.
- **Access**: Free usage is limited; after reaching the limit, users can wait or upgrade. Official help lists Power at $20/month with 500M tokens/week and Maximum at $100/month with 3B tokens/week. The free tier's exact weekly token allowance **needs verification**.
- **What it is not**: Several other AI products and devices are named “Muse” — this topic concerns only **Meta's Muse app and muse.ai**.

## Card 3 — Dots (OpenAI)

- **Official announcement**: [openai.com — Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/) (automated reading blocked — browser verification needed).
- **What it is**: An always-on agent inside ChatGPT. Give it a goal, connect apps, and define what it may do independently; it works using its own cloud computer and browser and brings back results for review. More than 4,000 app connections (secondary source).
- **Approval**: Users define the scope of independent work, and results are brought back for review.
- **Access**: Exclusive to ChatGPT **Pro and Business Premium**. The first dot is included in the plan. Usage does not count against plan limits for the first month after launch.
- **What it is not**: Different from ChatGPT's ordinary Agent mode and Codex itself — Dots is a separate feature that keeps working in the background regardless of device or screen.

## Card 4 — Instinct (Spear Street Technology)

- **Official page**: [instinct.com](https://instinct.com) — secondary sources also mention instinct.co and app.instinct.com, so verify the official address before signing up (M1 principle: beware of similarly named products).
- **What it is**: The homepage describes “a personal assistant that understands what you're working on and what's important to you.” Tasks assigned by text or phone include schedule management, email organization, car and flight bookings, and other personal work. A phone concierge feature was reported on 2026-09-16 (secondary).
- **Approval/risks**: Agency clause (commitments made on your behalf bind you); training opt-out available with exceptions for safety-review materials; disconnecting does not remove already indexed material, which requires a separate deletion process — **verified in official terms and privacy policy (2026-10-09)**. Training enabled by default, collected fields (email, messages, precise location), and a demonstrated phishing case via email are **secondary-source claims**.
- **Access**: Invitation only — waitlist or invitation from an existing user. Ages 18+. Free during beta; regular pricing undisclosed.
- **In this topic**: Changsoo Park joined the waitlist (Oct 9). Research and comparison only until approval; after approval, begin with public-information tasks **without connecting personal accounts**.
- **Sources (secondary)**: [Layer3 Labs — What Is Instinct AI?](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/instinct-ai-explained) — [Vellum — Official Instinct Breakdown](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/official-instinct-breakdown) — [eesel — Instinct AI review](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/instinct-ai-review) — [gyld — privacy risks](https://gyld.ai/blog/instinct-ai-review-impressive-agent-real-privacy-risks)
