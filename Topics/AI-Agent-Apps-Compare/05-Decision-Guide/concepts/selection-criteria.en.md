# Agent Selection Criteria

[한국어](selection-criteria.md)

## Four Checks Before Choosing

1. **Data sensitivity**: Determine whether public, synthetic, or personal/customer materials are needed. If public or fictional materials suffice for a test, use them instead of real data.
2. **Action impact**: Distinguish reading, summarizing, and drafting from writing, sending, changing, or deleting in external services. Keep actions that are hard to reverse or affect others as drafts until a person reviews and approves them.
3. **Actual access conditions**: Availability varies by region, plan, invitation, and connector permissions. Do not recommend a researched-only service as though you had used it directly.
4. **How to verify results**: Decide before starting whether quality will be judged by correct answers, sources, format, or editing effort. If accuracy cannot be independently verified, limit the task to a reviewable draft rather than automatic execution.

## Levels of Practice Evidence

- **Direct practice**: Results observed in the actual interface, such as Muse in M3/M4. The sample is small; do not generalize to all tasks.
- **Official-material verification**: Documentary evidence for product features, pricing, and access conditions. This does not prove usability or output quality.
- **Research only**: Dots, Grok Bot, and Instinct within this topic's scope. This is not a performance comparison using real tasks in those tools.

For current tool-specific evidence and constraints, see the [M2 comparison](../../02-Comparison-Table/guides/comparison-table.en.md#practice-decisions-for-this-topic); for evaluation evidence from the synthetic tasks actually run, see [M4 results](../../04-Real-Task-Trial/guides/trial-results.en.md#results-table).
