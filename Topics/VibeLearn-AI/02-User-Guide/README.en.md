## Key Insights (The "Aha!" Moments of This Module)

1. **The nature of the entry barrier**: Not technical difficulty (GitHub, setup) but anxiety about "am I doing this right?"
2. **The power of case studies**: "9.5 hours to a YouTube video" — one sentence replaces all persuasion
3. **The FAQ pattern**: 90% of first-time users ask the same 12 questions
4. **The key to the 30-minute guide**: Continuity from "minimum steps" to "first success experience"
