# Washington State Recording Law Study

> Korean version: [README.md](README.md)

## Overview

This folder contains the English guide for the Korean study materials on Washington State recording and privacy law, especially RCW 9.73 and the all-party consent rule. The materials focus on practical recording and video scenarios such as PKM workflows, content creation, work meetings, online meetings, and seminar recordings.

This README describes the English-facing structure. The original Korean content is available through [README.md](README.md), and translated English files use the `_en.md` suffix.

- [topic_info_en.md](topic_info_en.md) — Topic overview, learning goals, scope, references, and study environment
- [WA-Recording-Law-Study_en.md](WA-Recording-Law-Study_en.md) — Comprehensive study document covering Washington recording law principles, exceptions, and scenario-based judgments
