# Analysis of Key RCW 9.73.030 Provisions

## Prohibitions: What Is Prohibited?

```text
What RCW 9.73.030 prohibits:

(a) Private communications through electronic means such as telephone or telegraph
    → Recording or intercepting is prohibited without the consent of all participants

(b) Private conversations through any device
    → Recording or intercepting is prohibited without the consent of all people participating in the conversation
```

**Key word**: "private" — conversations with a reasonable expectation of privacy are protected.

## Four Exceptions: When Recording Is Allowed Without Consent

| # | Exception | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① | **Emergency situations** | Fire report, medical emergency, crime scene report, disaster |
| ② | **Threats, extortion, or threatening communications** | A call in which the other party threatens or intimidates you |
| ③ | **Anonymous or repeated harassing calls** | Unknown numbers repeatedly calling and using abusive language |
| ④ | **Hostage situations** | Police hostage negotiation calls |

> ⚠️ Recording workplace mistreatment for evidence does not fall under an exception.
> Exception ② may apply only when the other party makes threatening or intimidating statements.

## Journalist Exception

Journalists affiliated with regular news organizations may record without consent when the recording device is **readily apparent**.

> ⚠️ This does **not automatically apply** to YouTubers, bloggers, or ordinary individuals.

## Penalties

### Criminal Penalties (RCW 9.73.080)

```text
Offense: Gross misdemeanor
Penalty: Up to 364 days in jail + up to $5,000 fine
```

### Civil Penalties (RCW 9.73.060)

```text
Amounts a victim may claim:
- Actual damages, including emotional distress
- Or $100/day, up to $1,000
- Plus attorney fees

Criminal and civil proceedings may occur at the same time
```

### Evidentiary Use

Illegal recordings are **not admissible as evidence** in court.
