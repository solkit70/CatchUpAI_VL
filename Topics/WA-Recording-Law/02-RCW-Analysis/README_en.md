## 🎯 What You Learn in This Module

- What RCW 9.73.030 prohibits: private communications + private conversations
- Four exceptions: emergencies, threats, harassment, and hostage situations
- Penalties: criminal (gross misdemeanor) + civil ($100/day)
- The legal difference between public places and private spaces
- The legal difference between video recording and audio recording
