# VibeLearn AI Topic Starter

## 📌 Basic Topic Information

Topic name: WA-Recording-Law

Description: Study Washington State laws related to video/audio recording and audio recording, especially RCW 9.73. The goal is to understand what is legal and illegal in real-life scenarios such as PKM, content creation, work meetings, and school lectures, and then derive practical guidelines.

Learning purposes:
- Understand the legal standards for safely using ambient recording tools such as Limitless AI Recorder
- Understand the legally permitted scope of content creation activities such as YouTube filming and seminar recording
- Learn the procedures that must be followed when recording work meetings and online meetings
- Create practical guidelines for safely using audio/video recording in PKM activities
- (M6 addition) Understand how AI technology developments such as voice cloning, always-on recording devices, and AI bots affect recording law
- (M6 addition) Understand federal and Washington State legislative trends and the positions of companies and civil society

Expected duration: 1 day of focused learning (M1-M5 completed on 2026-05-31) + M6 Research 90 minutes (not completed)
