# Washington State Video and Audio Recording Law Study

> **Learning purpose**: Understand whether everyday audio/video recording activities such as PKM, content creation, and meeting records are legally permitted in Washington State, and derive practical guidelines.
> **Reference law**: RCW 9.73 (Washington Privacy Act)
> **Reference material**: RCFP Reporters Recording Guide — Washington

---

## 1. Core Principle: All-Party Consent

Washington State has one of the strictest recording consent standards in the United States.

| Category | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Federal law (Federal Wiretap Act)** | **One-party consent** — recording is allowed if one participant in the conversation, including yourself, consents |
| **Washington State law (RCW 9.73.030)** | **All-party consent** — consent is required from **every** person participating in the conversation |
| **Conclusion** | Washington State is **much stricter** than federal law. Even if something is legal under federal law, it may be illegal in Washington State |

> 💡 About 12 of the 50 U.S. states are all-party consent states. The rest are one-party consent states.

---

## 2. Main Law: RCW 9.73.030

### Basic Prohibitions

It is **illegal** to record or intercept the following:

- **(a)** **Private communications** through telephone, telegraph, radio, or other electronic communication means — "without the consent of all participants"
- **(b)** **Private conversations** through any recording or transmitting device — "without the consent of all persons engaged in the conversation"

### Forms of Consent

There are two ways to obtain consent:

1. **Explicit consent**: directly asking each person for consent
2. **Constructive consent by notice**: notifying all other parties before recording, in a reasonably effective way, that the conversation is being recorded. If they continue the conversation afterward, they are considered to have consented

```text
Example: Say "This conversation is being recorded."
If the other person continues the conversation, consent is established.
```

### Key Term: "Private Communication"

The law protects **private communications**. In other words, conversations in situations where there is no **reasonable expectation of privacy** may not be covered by this law.

## 3. Exceptions: When Recording May Be Allowed Without Consent

RCW 9.73.030 section (2) defines exceptions:

| Exception situation | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Emergency situations** | Related to fire, medical emergency, crime, or disaster reporting |
| **Threats, extortion, or intimidation** | Communications that include unlawful conduct by the other party |
| **Anonymous, repeated, or harassing calls** | Anonymous calls, repeated calls, or calls at inconvenient hours |
| **Hostage situations** | Situations related to barricades or hostages |

### Journalist Exception

Journalists affiliated with regular news organizations may record without consent if one of the following is satisfied:
- Consent was explicitly given
- The recording device is clearly visible or obvious to the speaker, meaning it is readily apparent

> ⚠️ This exception applies only to journalists affiliated with regular news media. It does not automatically apply to ordinary YouTubers or bloggers.

## 4. Public Places vs. Private Spaces

### Core Standard: Reasonable Expectation of Privacy

```text
High expectation of privacy → consent required
No expectation of privacy → recording may be allowed, but conversation content still requires separate judgment
```

| Location | Privacy expectation | Recording allowed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public streets/parks | None | Generally allowed for filming/recording |
| Public rallies/protests | None | Allowed |
| Restaurants/cafes, public statements | Low | Mostly allowed |
| Seminars/lectures, public events | Low | Mostly allowed, but organizer policy should be checked |
| Office/conference rooms | High | **Consent required** |
| Private residences | Very high | **Consent always required** |
| Online meetings | High | **Consent required** |
| School classrooms | Medium, case law unclear | **Consent recommended** |

> ⚠️ Important: Video filming in public places is generally legal, but audio recording **private conversations** within that space requires a separate legal judgment.

## 5. Scenario Analysis

### 🎙️ Limitless AI Recorder — All-Day Ambient Recording

**Risk level: Very high**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Solo monologues or notes | ✅ Legal — no other party |
| Conversation with family or housemates | ⚠️ Consent required — private communication |
| Conversation with coworkers | ❌ Illegal without consent |
| Collecting other people's conversations in public | ❌ Clearly illegal |
| Conversation with store/cafe staff | ⚠️ Consent recommended |

**Practical solutions**:
- Before a conversation starts, say "This conversation is being recorded" and continue only if the other party accepts
- Review whether the Limitless app has an automatic recording notice feature
- Use it only for your own monologues, thinking, and notes when alone

---

### 📹 YouTubers/Creators — Outdoor Filming and Seminar Recording

**Risk level: Depends on situation**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Filming scenery or events in outdoor public places | ✅ Legal |
| Filming public protests or rallies | ✅ Legal, protected by the First Amendment |
| Filming faces of people on the street | ✅ Legal because there is no privacy expectation in public places |
| Recording main presentation at a public seminar | ✅ Mostly legal, but organizer policy must be checked |
| Unauthorized recording at a private or paid seminar | ❌ Contract violation + legal risk |
| Recording private conversations among attendees | ❌ Illegal without consent |
| Recording the entire event with organizer consent | ✅ Legal |

**Recommended procedure**:
1. Before the seminar starts, ask the organizer, "May I record this?"
2. Check whether attendees were notified that the event will be filmed or recorded
3. If there is no notice, obtain consent individually where needed

### 🎓 Recording School Lectures for Review

**Risk level: Medium, depending on school policy**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Recording after getting permission from the professor | ✅ Legal |
| Recording secretly without the professor knowing | ⚠️ Legally unclear and may violate school policy |
| Screen-recording online classes | ⚠️ Most schools prohibit it by policy, and copyright issues exist |
| Recording as an ADA accommodation | ✅ Legal if officially approved |

**Practical solution**:
- Ask the professor by email at the beginning of the semester
- Clearly state that it is for personal review only
- Most professors allow personal review recordings

---

### 💻 Online Meetings — Zoom, Teams, Google Meet

**Risk level: Low if platform features are used**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom host uses official recording feature | ✅ Legal — automatic notice is sent to participants |
| Participant secretly screen-records | ❌ Illegal in Washington State |
| Notice before start: "This meeting is being recorded" | ✅ Legal |
| AI note tools such as Otter.ai | ⚠️ Must notify all participants beforehand |

**Safe online meeting recording method in Washington State**:

```text
① At the start of the meeting: verbally say "This meeting is being recorded"
② In chat: post "This meeting is being recorded"
③ If you are the Zoom host: use the built-in recording feature, which automatically notifies participants
```

### 🏢 Company/Work Meetings

**Risk level: Medium, depending on notice**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Notice before meeting: "I am recording" | ✅ Legal |
| Secret recording for evidence | ❌ Illegal in Washington State, unless an exception applies |
| Recording a 1:1 meeting without the other party's consent | ❌ Illegal |
| Conference room AI speaker automatically records | ⚠️ Notice to all participants required |

> ⚠️ Washington State is one of the strictest states regarding secret workplace recordings. In some other states, one-party consent may allow recording of conversations you participate in, but Washington does not.

---

### 📱 PKM Use — Recording → AI Transcript → Knowledge Management

**Core point**: The legality of a recording depends on **the act of recording itself**, not on the purpose of storage or use.

- Personal notes or monologues → ✅ Legal
- Recording with the other party's consent → ✅ Legal, and AI processing/storage are also possible
- Recording without consent → ❌ Illegal, even if used only for personal PKM later

## 6. Penalties

### Criminal Penalties (RCW 9.73.080)

| Violation | Penalty |
| --- | --- |
| Violation of RCW 9.73.030, such as unauthorized recording | **Gross misdemeanor** |
| Illegal publication or alteration of recordings | **Gross misdemeanor** |

**Gross misdemeanor penalty level**: up to 364 days in jail + up to $5,000 fine

### Civil Penalties (RCW 9.73.060)

| Category | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Actual damages | Actual harm, including emotional distress |
| Liquidated damages | **$100/day**, up to **$1,000** |
| Attorney fees | Paid by the opposing party if the plaintiff prevails |

> ⚠️ Criminal penalties and civil lawsuits may occur **at the same time**.

## 7. Practical Guidelines Summary

### Seven Principles for Safe Recording

```text
1. Always give notice first
   "This conversation/meeting/lecture is being recorded" → consent is established if the conversation continues

2. Record freely when alone
   Monologues, notes, and personal thinking recordings are not a problem

3. Check first for public events
   Organizer recording policy → OK if there is notice; ask if there is no notice

4. Use platform features for online meetings
   Zoom built-in recording → automatically notifies all participants

5. Always notify at work
   Verbal notice + chat notice at the beginning of work meetings

6. Get professor permission at school
   Ask by email at the beginning of the semester and state the review purpose

7. Use ambient recorders such as Limitless carefully
   Public scenery is OK, but recording other people's private conversations is not
```

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I record all day with Limitless AI and use it only for personal PKM?**
A: No. Washington State judges the **act** of recording, not the purpose. Recording conversations with others without consent is illegal regardless of the intended use.

**Q: Is filming with a camera in a public place legal?**
A: Video filming itself is generally legal. However, capturing specific private conversations as **audio** may be illegal.

**Q: Can I secretly record if the other person threatens or harasses me?**
A: Yes, in that case an exception may apply. Communications that include threats, extortion, or intimidation may be recorded under a one-party consent exception.

**Q: What if I capture an online class for review?**
A: School policy and instructor consent are required. If prohibited by policy, it can create legal risk and copyright issues.

**Q: What if I record a meeting while traveling outside Washington State?**
A: The law of that state applies. For example, California is also an all-party consent state, while New York is a one-party consent state.
