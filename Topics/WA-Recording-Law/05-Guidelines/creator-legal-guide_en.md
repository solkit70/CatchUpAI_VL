## 🔑 Core Principle: The Starting Point for Every Judgment

```text
Washington State = ALL-PARTY CONSENT

If you record a private conversation or communication
without the consent of all people participating in the conversation,
it is illegal.

Even if federal law's one-party consent standard is satisfied,
it may still be illegal in Washington State.
State law controls.
```

**The easiest way to obtain consent**: Before the conversation, say "This conversation is being recorded." If the other person continues the conversation, consent is established through constructive consent.

## ❓ Creator Q&A

---

### Q1. Is it okay if passersby appear in my YouTube video while I am filming outdoors?

**A: ✅ Generally legal**

Filming in public places is protected by the First Amendment. People in public places have no expectation of privacy, so appearing in the video is generally not a legal problem.

**Cautions**:
- Intentionally capturing the content of a specific person's private conversation as audio is a separate issue
- Sensitive places such as hospital entrances or courthouses may have separate rules
- If the recorded person did not consent to commercial use, likeness/right-of-publicity issues may arise separately from recording law

---

### Q2. Can I record a seminar I attend and review it later?

**A: ⚠️ Depends on organizer policy**

**Allowed when**:
- The organizer explicitly says filming/recording is allowed at the public event
- You obtained permission from the organizer in advance

**Not allowed when**:
- The organizer announced "no recording" → legal risk + breach of contract
- Unauthorized recording at a non-public or paid private event → illegal

**Practical recommendation**: Before the seminar starts, ask the organizer, "May I record this for personal review purposes?" Most organizers allow recording for personal review.

---

### Q3. Can I keep Limitless AI Recorder on all day and use surrounding content for PKM?

**A: ❌ Illegal risk when talking with others**

| Situation | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Monologue/notes when alone | ✅ Legal |
| Organizing thoughts while driving alone | ✅ Legal |
| Conversation with family/friends without consent | ❌ Illegal |
| Conversation with coworkers without consent | ❌ Illegal |
| Collecting ambient sound in public | ⚠️ Risky if others' conversations are captured |

**How to use it safely**:
1. Before a conversation starts, tell the other person, "I am recording with Limitless right now"
2. Consider configuring the app to automatically play a recording notice at the start of conversations
3. Enable ambient recording only when you are alone

---

### Q4. Can I record online meetings such as Zoom or Teams?

**A: Conditionally legal — the method matters**

| Method | Judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom host uses built-in recording feature | ✅ Legal (the platform automatically notifies all participants) |
| Verbal notice at the start: "This meeting is being recorded" | ✅ Legal |
| Chat notice: "This meeting is being recorded" | ✅ Legal (supporting measure) |
| Screen-recording without the host/participants knowing | ❌ Illegal |
| Connecting an AI note tool such as Otter.ai | ⚠️ Prior notice to all participants required |

**Safest method**: At the start of the meeting, verbally say "This meeting is being recorded," and use Zoom's built-in recording feature.

---

### Q5. Can I record a school lecture for review?

**A: ⚠️ Professor consent required**

Under Washington State law, the professor is also a conversation participant, so recording without consent creates illegal risk. School policy may impose additional restrictions.

**Practical recommendation**:
- At the beginning of the semester, ask the professor by email for permission to record, specifying review purposes
- Most professors allow recording for personal review purposes
- If officially approved as an ADA accommodation, it is clearly legal

---

### Q6. Can I record meetings at work?

**A: ❌ Illegal without prior notice**

Washington State is one of the strictest states regarding secret workplace recordings.

**Legal methods**:
- At the start of the meeting, verbally announce "This meeting is being recorded"
- For recurring meetings, state in the calendar invite that "meetings are recorded"

**Caution**: Even if a manager makes unfair remarks, secretly recording is illegal in Washington State. However, if the other party makes threatening or intimidating statements, an exception may apply.

---

### Q7. Can I record a seminar and upload it to YouTube?

**A: ❌ Risk of dual violations**

1. **Recording law violation**: no consent from organizer, speaker, or attendees
2. **Copyright infringement**: presentation content is the speaker's copyrighted work

**Legal method**: Obtain official filming authority through an agreement with the organizer + signed consent from the speaker.

---

### Q8. Is it okay if other people's conversations are captured in video from a public event?

**A: ⚠️ Depends on the situation**

If the audio is naturally captured as background noise, it is generally not a problem. However, if a specific private conversation is clearly captured, it is risky.

**Practical tip**: During video editing, the safest approach is to mute identifiable private conversation segments.

---

### Q9. What if I record a YouTube video or online lecture for personal study?

**A: ⚠️ Copyright issue + platform terms of service**

This raises copyright law issues separate from recording law. Even personal use can violate platform terms of service. Legally, it is a gray area, and commercial use is clearly problematic.

## ✅ Seven Principles for Safe Recording

```text
1. Always give notice first
   One sentence, "This conversation is being recorded," establishes constructive consent

2. When you are alone, record freely
   Monologues, notes, and personal reflections are unrestricted

3. Ask the organizer first for public events
   "May I record this?" — usually OK, and keep a record

4. Use built-in platform features for online meetings
   Zoom recording button = automatic consent notice = safest

5. Always give notice in work meetings
   Verbal notice at the start + text notice in chat

6. Get professor permission at school
   Ask by email and specify review purpose

7. Use ambient recorders such as Limitless carefully
   Keep them on only when alone, or always notify conversation partners
```

## 📞 Notice Language

```text
For work meetings:
"This meeting is being recorded."

For casual conversations:
"Just so you know, I am recording right now. Is that okay?"

For seminar/event advance confirmation:
"May I record today's event for personal review purposes?"

For online meeting chat notice:
"This meeting is being recorded for [purpose]. Please let me know if you have any concerns."
```
