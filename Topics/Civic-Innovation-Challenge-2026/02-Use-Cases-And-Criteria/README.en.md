# M2 — The 8 Use Cases and the Evaluation Rubric

**Status**: ✅ Complete (Oct 24, 2026) · **Reading time**: 30 minutes · [한국어](README.md)

> **In one line**: Applicants choose, from the agencies' **8 use cases** (5 involve AI), a problem their solution helps with, and show the **9 rubric criteria** through the **16 application questions**.

## 📚 Reading order

1. [concepts/use-cases.en.md](concepts/use-cases.en.md) — Cards for the 8 use cases by agency: problem, what they want, good fit for, watch out
2. [concepts/rubric-map.en.md](concepts/rubric-map.en.md) — The 9 rubric criteria mapped to the 16 questions, and what is new since round 1 (2025)
3. [guides/office-hours-questions.md](guides/office-hours-questions.md) — Questions to ask at the Oct 26, 2026 in-person office hours in Tukwila (English questions with Korean notes and an answer column)

## Key points

- The agencies set the topics. Ideas outside the use cases are open only under **King County's policy goals**.
- **KCIT use cases require vendor registration** in the King County supplier portal.
- **Product maturity has no dedicated question**, so state it yourself in the overview and milestone answers.
- Round 2 looks much more closely than round 1 at **real deployability** (implementation, cost, use across agencies).
