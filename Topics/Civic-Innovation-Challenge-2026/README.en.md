## The program in three sentences

1. **What**: A pilot program in which the City of Bellevue, King County, Washington State and Bellevue College test technology from startups and individuals on real problems in their own work, over a few months (run by the Exchange Northwest).
2. **Who**: Individuals, teams, start-ups and businesses aged 18+ that **have — or are building —** a solution to a public need. **4–6 recipients**, stipend **$15,000–$45,000** (most at the lower end).
3. **When**: Applications due Nov 13, 2026; selected teams work January–July 2027.

## What we found

- **This is not a prize contest; it is a pilot with public partners.** You test and refine your solution with agency staff, and **the pilot does not guarantee adoption or a contract** (official FAQ).
- **The stipend range is wide, but the call says "most at the lower end."** About $15,000 is a realistic expectation.
- **An idea alone is unlikely to be enough.** You need a solution in hand or in progress and a 2-minute pitch video; finalists provide a tax ID and a certificate of insurance.
- **All four round-1 (2025) teams attached a working AI product to a specific agency workflow in a small pilot.** Teams from Seattle, Denver and Israel were selected, and so was an app built by a high school student.
- **Round 2 looks much more closely at real deployability.** The rubric grew from 4 to 9 criteria, adding product maturity, number of jurisdictions served and metrics, and the form now asks for an implementation plan and a cost proposal.
- **Easy points to miss**: product maturity has no dedicated question, so state it in the overview and milestone answers; King County use cases require vendor registration.
