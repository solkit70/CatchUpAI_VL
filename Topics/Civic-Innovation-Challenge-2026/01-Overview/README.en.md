# M1 — At a Glance: What, Who, When

**Status**: ✅ Complete (Oct 24, 2026) · **Reading time**: 20 minutes · [한국어](README.md)

> **In three sentences**
> 1. **What**: A pilot program in which the City of Bellevue, King County, Washington State and Bellevue College test technology from startups and individuals on real problems in their own work, over a few months.
> 2. **Who**: Individuals, teams, start-ups and businesses aged 18+ that **have — or are building —** a solution to a public need. **4–6 recipients**, stipend **$15,000–$45,000** (most at the lower end).
> 3. **By when**: Applications due **Friday, November 13, 2026, 5:00 PM Pacific Time**; selected teams work January–July 2027.

## Key points

- The pilot **does not guarantee adoption**. It is a chance to test and refine your solution with public partners.
- The stipend range is wide, but the call says **"most solutions will likely receive a stipend at the lower end."**
- **An idea alone is unlikely to be enough.** You need a solution in hand or in progress, plus a 2-minute pitch video, a tax ID and insurance.
- Eligibility details not in the call (U.S. entity, residency, insurance level) will be checked at the Oct 26 office hours and updated in Module 4.
