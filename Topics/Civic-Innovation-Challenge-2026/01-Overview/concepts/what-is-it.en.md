# What Is the Civic Innovation Challenge?

> **In three sentences**
> 1. **What**: A pilot program in which the City of Bellevue, King County, Washington State and Bellevue College test technology from startups and individuals on real problems in their own work, over a few months.
> 2. **Who**: Individuals, teams, start-ups and businesses aged 18+ that **have — or are building —** a solution to a public need can apply; **4–6 recipients** will be selected and receive a **$15,000–$45,000** stipend.
> 3. **By when**: Applications are due **Friday, November 13, 2026, 5:00 PM Pacific Time**; selected teams work from January to July 2027.

## Who runs it — The Exchange Northwest

The program is run by **the Exchange Northwest**, a regional partnership formed in April 2026 by the City of Bellevue, Bellevue College, King County Information Technology (KCIT) and Washington Technology Solutions (WaTech). It focuses on civic innovation, digital access and community-driven technology solutions. The first round (2025) was run by the City of Bellevue alone; in this second round the partnership expands to King County, Washington State and Bellevue College.[^overview][^launch]

## What happens — a PoC pilot with public partners

Selected participants work with government or education staff (**civic design partners**) to validate their solution **in real workflows**, gather feedback and refine it. The call describes this as a timebound **Proof-of-Concept (PoC)** phase with "light weekly engagement" from both sides.[^overview]

A design partner is a **co-designer, not a buyer**. The FAQ states clearly that **the pilot does not automatically lead to product adoption or any further commitment**. Instead, participants receive a summary of findings and recommendations, results may be published with their permission, and there is an opportunity to showcase at the annual Exchange Northwest event in May or June 2027.[^faq]

## What you get — four benefits

| # | Benefit | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coaching and mentoring | Seasoned entrepreneurs, advisors and investors |
| 2 | Civic design partnership | Test, optimize and refine with City of Bellevue, KCIT, WaTech and/or Bellevue College staff |
| 3 | Showcase and network | Present to a civic tech audience; build relationships across the ecosystem |
| 4 | **Stipend** | **$15,000–$45,000** |

The stipend depends on the complexity of the solution, **whether it applies to more than one government or education partner**, and product maturity. The call notes that **"most solutions will likely receive a stipend at the lower end of the scale,"** so about $15,000 is a realistic expectation. Stipends are paid by the participating partner agency.[^overview]

## How many are selected

The second round expects to select **4–6 recipients**.[^overview] For reference, the first round (2025) received **23 applications and selected 4**.[^round1]
