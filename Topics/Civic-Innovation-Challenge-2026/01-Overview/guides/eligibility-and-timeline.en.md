# Who Can Apply, and What Happens When

## 1. Eligibility

### Official eligibility — the three conditions in the call

| Condition | Wording in the call | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Who | individuals, teams, start-ups and businesses | **Individuals, teams, start-ups and businesses** — you do not need a company |
| Age | at least 18 years of age | **18 or older** |
| Authority | authorized to sign a contract on behalf of themselves or their company | You can **sign a contract** for yourself or your company |

Source: Overview[^overview]

### Practically required — conditions spread across the documents

| Requirement | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Have or be building a solution** | Use Cases[^usecases] | Applicants "should have — or be in the process of building — technology or business solutions." An idea alone is unlikely to be enough |
| Complete application | Submission[^submission] | 16 questions, 175 words each. Incomplete applications are not evaluated |
| 2-minute pitch video | Submission, question 16 | A video link is required |
| Cost proposal | Submission, question 14 | Total pilot cost and future costs |
| Tax ID | Submission (finalists) | **W-9 or TIN** before signing an agreement |
| Insurance | Submission (finalists) | **Certificate of Insurance** |
| Vendor registration | Submission · Use Cases | May need a supplier portal account; **KCIT use cases require registration in the King County E-Procurement Supplier Portal** |
| Time | FAQ[^faq] | **About 3–5 hours per week** over roughly 30–35 weeks |

### Not stated in the call — to be confirmed at the Oct 26 office hours

- **Residency or company location** — not stated.
- **Whether a U.S. entity is needed** — not required in writing, but finalists submit a W-9 (a U.S. taxpayer form), so **a U.S. tax ID is likely needed in practice** (our inference).
- **Insurance coverage level** — not specified.
- **Whether concept-stage products can be selected** — "concept only" is a level in the rubric, but actual selection chances are unknown.

> We will update this section after the in-person office hours at Tukwila on October 26, 2026 (Module 4).
