## Key points

- **Product maturity has no dedicated question** — state your stage and evidence in questions 4 and 10.
- **Give question 13's metrics in numbers**, and take question 11 **beyond the pilot to deployment**.
- **Keep question 14's cost realistic** — most stipends are at the lower end.
- **Write one sentence on what you want to prove with the government in six months** — the advice stressed most at the office hours.
- **Out-of-state teams and prototype-stage products both qualify** (Oct 26). Non-U.S. teams, insurance level and whether support letters count were not confirmed, so follow the call.
