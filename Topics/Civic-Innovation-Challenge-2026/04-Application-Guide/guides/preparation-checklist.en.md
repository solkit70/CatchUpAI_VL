# Preparation Checklist and Backward Timeline

This plan counts backward from the deadline: **Friday, November 13, 2026, 5:00 PM Pacific Time**. The call **strongly encourages submitting early**, so the team can reach out if documentation is missing.[^submission]

## 1. First — should you apply? (5-minute check)

| # | Check | Yes / No |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You are 18+ and can sign a contract for yourself or your company | |
| 2 | One of the 8 use cases (or King County's policy goals) matches a problem your solution helps with | |
| 3 | Your solution **exists or is being built** (at least a prototype you can demo) | |
| 4 | You can commit **3–5 hours a week** for about 30–35 weeks | |
| 5 | If you become a finalist, you can provide a **tax ID (W-9/TIN)** and a **certificate of insurance** | |

If any answer is "no," read [4. When applying is hard](#4-when-applying-is-hard) first. Details for items 3 and 5 (whether concept-stage works, whether a U.S. entity is needed) are **being confirmed at the Oct 26 office hours**.

## 3. Final check before submitting

- [ ] All 16 questions answered, each within 175 words
- [ ] Question 4 says **which agency and why**
- [ ] Questions 4 and 10 state **your product's current stage** (users, pilot results) — maturity has no dedicated question
- [ ] Question 5 fits **five months** with 1–2 hrs/week from the design partner and 2–3 hrs/month from a mentor
- [ ] Questions 8 and 12 describe **community benefit and partner organizations**
- [ ] Question 11 describes **pilot-to-full-deployment** steps
- [ ] Question 13 gives **metrics in numbers**
- [ ] Question 14's cost is realistic (most stipends are at the lower end)
- [ ] Question 16's video link opens, with any password included
- [ ] You received a submission confirmation

## 4. When applying is hard

| Situation | What you can do this round |
|---|---|
| **You only have an idea** | Solutions must exist or be in progress. See whether you can build a demo-able prototype by the deadline; if not, **join a team** that has a solution or prepare for the **next round** |
| **No U.S. tax ID** | Finalists submit a W-9 or TIN. Whether non-U.S. teams or residents can apply is **being confirmed at the office hours** |
| **Under 18** | Unlike round 1, round 2 requires 18+. Whether you can join a team led by an adult is **being confirmed** |
| **Not enough time** | The pilot (Jan–Jul 2027) needs 3–5 hours a week. See whether your team can share the work |
| **Your topic doesn't match a use case** | Check King County's policy goals (homelessness, housing, community, transparent government). If none fit, wait for the next round's use cases |
