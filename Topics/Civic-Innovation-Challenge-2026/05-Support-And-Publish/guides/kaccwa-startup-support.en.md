# KACCWA Startup Support Team — How We Can Help

**As of**: Oct 29, 2026 · [한국어](kaccwa-startup-support.md)

At its board meeting on October 28, 2026, the Korean-American Chamber of Commerce of Washington State (KACCWA) decided to **spread the word about this program to its members and the community, and to have its Startup Support Team help applicants who want support**. The Chamber is not applying itself, because eligibility is for individuals and companies with a product or service that addresses the use cases.

## Our position

- **You are welcome to apply on your own.** Applications are made by each individual or company.
- If you want or need help, **the Startup Support Team will do its best to support you.**
- We do not write applications for you or promise selection.

To understand the program first, see Modules [M1](../../01-Overview/README.en.md) to [M4](../../04-Application-Guide/README.en.md) of this Topic.
